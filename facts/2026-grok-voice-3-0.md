## What happened
On October 14, 2026, SpaceXAI makes grok-voice-3.0 available with Speech to Speech in its API. The grok-voice-latest alias now points to it, so sessions that use the alias upgrade automatically, and the API itself is unchanged.

## Why it matters
The grok-voice-latest alias moves to the new model, so developers who use the alias get it without changing their code.
