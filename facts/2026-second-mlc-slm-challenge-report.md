## What happened
On September 23, 2026, Bingshen Mu and colleagues posted their report on the second Multilingual Conversational Speech Language Model Challenge at Interspeech. The organizers used about 2,100 hours of two-person conversations spanning 14 languages and 21 language and accent variants. One task asked systems to track who spoke when and transcribe each speaker. A separate task asked them to answer questions about the conversation and sounds in the recording. The organizers reported participation from 91 teams.

## Why it matters
A transcript can get the words right while assigning them to the wrong speaker. Evaluating speaker attribution, transcription and comprehension within the same challenge makes it easier to see where a speech model struggles across languages and accents.
