## What happened
OpenAI introduced DALL·E 2 on April 6, 2022 as a limited research preview. Given a caption, the model first predicts how CLIP would represent a matching image as a set of numbers. A diffusion model then uses that representation to turn random noise into an image in successive steps.

OpenAI began inviting people from the waitlist into the beta on July 20 and removed the waitlist on September 28. The developer API entered public beta on November 3.

## Why it matters
DALL·E 2 put CLIP’s learned connections between words and images to work inside the generation process. OpenAI reported that evaluators preferred its images to the original model’s for both realism and how well they matched the caption.
