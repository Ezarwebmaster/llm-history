## What happened
In September 1997, Philip Clarkson (Cambridge) and Ronald Rosenfeld (Carnegie Mellon) presented a new version of the CMU Statistical Language Modeling toolkit at the Eurospeech conference in Rhodes. The first version, released in 1994, let researchers build and test bigram and trigram models, which predict a word from the one or two words before it. The authors say more than 40 academic, government and industrial labs in over 12 countries used it. The paper describes the improved efficiency and added features of the new version.

## Why it matters
Free, shared tools like this let many labs build word-prediction models in the same way and compare their results.
