## What happened
On November 17, 2026, OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Sol (`gpt-6.2-sol`) as an efficient model for everyday coding, writing, code reviews, and multi-step workflows that use tools. Available at launch across ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the OpenAI API, it accepts text and image inputs, outputs text, and supports a 1,050,000-token context window (up to 922,000 input tokens and 128,000 output tokens) with configurable reasoning effort from low to max.

## Why it matters
OpenAI reports that GPT-6.2 Sol completes routine and agentic tasks while generating fewer output tokens, and at maximum reasoning effort closely approaches OpenAI's heavier flagship tier on FrontierCode 2.0, which grades whether real-codebase changes meet maintainers' merge standards.
