## What happened
AI21 Labs opened AI21 Studio, a developer platform, in open beta, giving free sign-up access to its Jurassic-1 models: Jumbo, at 178 billion parameters, and the smaller Large. AI21 Labs called Jumbo the largest and most sophisticated language model released for general developer use at the time. The model used a 250,000-token vocabulary, which AI21 Labs said was more than five times larger than most existing vocabularies and the first to include multi-word tokens such as names and common phrases, not just single word pieces.

## Why it matters
It showed that GPT-3-scale language models were no longer confined to one lab: a second company could train and ship one as a developer product.
