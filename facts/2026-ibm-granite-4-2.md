## What happened
On August 25, 2026, IBM released the Granite 4.2 family of language models in 3B, 8B and 30B sizes under the Apache 2.0 licence. IBM says they add "thinking" capabilities: the models reason step by step before answering, and are built for tool calling, coding and following long instructions. The same release introduced Granite Speech 5.0 Turbo CTC, a 470-million-parameter speech recognition model without a language-model backbone.

## Why it matters
IBM is betting that small, openly licensed models with explicit reasoning are enough for enterprise agents that run on a company's own machines, not just in a large cloud.
