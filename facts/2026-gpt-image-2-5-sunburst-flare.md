## What happened
On September 8, 2026, OpenAI released two API models alongside the ChatGPT Images 2.5 rollout. Their identifiers are `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` and `gpt-image-2.5-flare`, with dated snapshots ending in `-2026-09-08`. Both take text and image inputs and produce images.

OpenAI positioned Flare as the everyday choice, claiming higher image quality than GPT Image 2 at 50% lower latency. Sunburst offered greater editing precision for detailed creative work, with longer generation times.

## Why it matters
The release gave developers a choice between speed and tighter control across edits. The accompanying ChatGPT Images 2.5 experience added features such as Sketch and templates; those interface features were distinct from the two named API models.
