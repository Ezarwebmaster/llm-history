## What happened
On July 8, 2026, OpenAI began rolling out GPT-Live-1 and GPT-Live-1 mini to ChatGPT users globally. GPT-Live-1 became generally available in the API on September 10. It continuously processes incoming speech while generating speech, and can delegate search, reasoning and actions to a backend model while continuing the conversation.

## Why it matters
Full-duplex interaction allows the model to listen through interruptions and decide whether to speak, pause or keep listening. Earlier turn-based voice models, including the GPT-Realtime family, process speech in separate turns; Live separates continuous conversation from deeper work done by another model.
