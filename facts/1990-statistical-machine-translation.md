## What happened
Peter Brown and colleagues described a system that learns from French and English versions of Canadian parliamentary proceedings. It combines a model that estimates how likely an English sentence is with one that estimates how likely a French sentence is as its translation.

## Why it matters
The paper illustrates how language models could help choose between possible translations using patterns learned from text rather than relying only on hand-written rules.
