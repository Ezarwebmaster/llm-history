## What happened
OpenAI released GPT-Realtime-2.1 to the Realtime API, updating its reasoning voice model. OpenAI says it improved recognition of letters and numbers, handling of silence and noise, and behavior when interrupted. It retains configurable reasoning effort and tool use.

## Why it matters
These changes target practical voice-agent problems: capturing identifiers accurately, coping with imperfect audio, and managing a conversation when a person cuts in.
