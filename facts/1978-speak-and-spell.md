## What happened
In 1978, Texas Instruments introduced the Speak & Spell, a handheld electronic learning toy for children. It spoke words aloud through a custom chip, the first single-chip speech synthesizer and the first use of a digital signal processor (a chip built to crunch signals such as voice) to generate speech. A child typed a word, heard it spoken, and then spelled it back on the keyboard.

## Why it matters
The toy put synthesized speech into millions of homes, showed that one cheap chip could produce intelligible spoken words, and helped launch the digital signal processing industry behind today's voice assistants, modems and audio devices.
