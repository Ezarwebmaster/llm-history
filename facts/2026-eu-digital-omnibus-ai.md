## What happened
On July 24, 2026, the European Union published Regulation (EU) 2026/1744—the Digital Omnibus on AI—in its Official Journal, following adoption on July 8 and entering into force on July 27, 2026. The regulation amends the 2024 EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) along with EU aviation and machinery safety laws to ease compliance burdens and revise deadlines. Specifically, it postpones obligations for stand-alone high-risk AI systems under Annex III (such as hiring, credit scoring, education, and biometrics) from August 2, 2026 to December 2, 2027, and defers high-risk AI safety components embedded in regulated products under Annex I from August 2, 2027 to August 2, 2028, while leaving prohibitions and general-purpose AI model rules intact.

## Why it matters
Amending the EU AI Act days before its August 2026 milestone showed regulators balancing strict general-purpose AI oversight with longer transition windows for high-risk deployments.
