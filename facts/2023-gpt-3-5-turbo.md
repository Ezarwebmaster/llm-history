## What happened
OpenAI released `gpt-3.5-turbo`, the model used in ChatGPT, for developers on March 1, 2023. The chat API accepted a sequence of messages rather than a single unstructured text prompt. This was a separate event from the launch of the ChatGPT website on November 30, 2022.

## Why it matters
The API let other companies put conversational language models inside their own products. OpenAI named Snapchat's My AI, Quizlet's Q-Chat tutor and Shopify's shopping assistant as early uses, showing how the technology could move beyond OpenAI's own chat interface.
