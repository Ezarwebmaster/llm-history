## What happened
OpenAI introduced a benchmark of 99 problems testing whether models can interpret molecular structures and experimental evidence, explain results, select compounds and predict their properties. It evaluated GPT models at different reasoning settings.

## Why it matters
The tasks probe whether models can connect visual and experimental evidence in a scientific workflow. OpenAI cautioned that these retrospective tests do not establish whether models can discover drugs or improve laboratory decisions, and prior exposure to the published sources cannot be ruled out.
