## What happened
On June 15, 2026, Tokyo-based Sakana AI launched Fugu and its larger variant Fugu Ultra, a model family designed around multi-agent coordination and collective intelligence. Rather than answering long multi-step queries with a single monolithic model pass, Fugu acts as a central coordinator that decomposes complex goals into distinct planning, code-generation, and verification sub-tasks, routing each sub-task to the most suitable specialized model. Sakana AI published benchmarks showing that Fugu Ultra outperformed individual dense models across complex enterprise multi-step problem solving and planning benchmarks while significantly reducing overall inference cost.

## Why it matters
It reflects the industry transition from single monolithic foundation models toward agentic orchestration layers that coordinate pools of specialized models to solve complex tasks.
