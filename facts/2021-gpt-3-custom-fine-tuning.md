## What happened
OpenAI announced that developers could train a customized GPT-3 model on their own examples and use it through the API. The announcement described experiments fine-tuning two model sizes, Curie and Davinci, on grade-school mathematics. It also described applications including text generation, summarization and classification.

## Why it matters
Fine-tuning adjusts a base model using additional examples, giving developers another way to adapt it beyond putting examples in each prompt. This dated milestone documents the two models in use without assuming separate launch dates for them.
