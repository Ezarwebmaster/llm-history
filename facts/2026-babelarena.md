## What happened
The authors introduced BabelFlow, a workflow for adapting agent benchmarks to other languages, and used it to build BabelArena. Their tests found that lower-resource languages had distinct tool-use and control-flow failures, and no single model led across all benchmark families.

## Why it matters
Testing agents only in English can miss problems that appear when they carry out multi-step tasks in other languages.
