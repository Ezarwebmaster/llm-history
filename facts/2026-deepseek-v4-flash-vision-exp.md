## What happened
On 21 August 2026, DeepSeek put an experimental multimodal version of its V4 Flash model on the API platform. DeepSeek says it matches the text-only V4 Flash on agents, reasoning and world knowledge, and is substantially stronger on multimodal agent benchmarks, close to Opus 4.8 in its comparison. Images are tokenised and billed per image, and DeepSeek Harness 0.1.1 was released the same day with support for it.

## Why it matters
A cheap, fast model that can read images and use tools made visual agent work affordable through the same API, rather than reserving it for flagship models.
