## What happened
OpenAI announced GPT-Rosalind as a reasoning model for biology, drug discovery and translational medicine. Qualified customers could access it in ChatGPT, Codex and the API through trusted access. On September 11, OpenAI said it had left research preview and was available globally to eligible organizations.

## Why it matters
This established the specialized model family before the June GPT-Rosalind-5.5 update and the October GPT-6 Rosalind Research release. Access was controlled from the outset.
