## What happened
On October 15, 2026, Meta released the weights of Muse Spark Open, a natively multimodal sparse mixture-of-experts model — an architecture that activates a small slice of its weights for each token — with 816 billion total and 42 billion active parameters and a 1-million-token context window. Meta calls it the most capable US-built open-weight model to date. Meta says it is the first model in its class to run on a desktop, using two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios with the MLX framework; weights are on Hugging Face and cloud marketplaces, and API platforms serve it too.

## Why it matters
An open-weight model near frontier scale, whose small active slice lets a pair of desktop machines hold it, moves self-hosted AI closer to what only big labs' APIs offered.
