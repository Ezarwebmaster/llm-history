## What happened
On 12 July 2022 the BigScience workshop released BLOOM, a 176-billion-parameter language model that generates text in 46 natural languages and 13 programming languages. Hugging Face, which hosted the project, called it the first multilingual large language model trained in full transparency and published the weights under a Responsible AI License so researchers could download, run and study it. The final training run took 117 days on the Jean Zay supercomputer in France, ending on 6 July, and involved more than 1,000 researchers from over 250 institutions.

## Why it matters
Before BLOOM, only a few well-funded industrial labs could build and inspect a model this large. An openly licensed, multilingual model let universities and smaller labs study a frontier-scale language model themselves, and gave languages other than English a strong model of their own.
