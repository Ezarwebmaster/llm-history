## What happened
On June 23, 2022, an OpenAI cluster outage took `code-cushman-001` and `code-davinci-001` offline; OpenAI identified both as Codex beta models. Its later deprecation table lists `code-cushman-001` and `code-cushman-002` among the original Codex models shut down on March 23, 2023, alongside the separate `code-davinci-001` and `code-davinci-002` IDs.

## Why it matters
The Cushman names denote code-oriented models in the original Codex API, distinct from the Davinci names. The outage documents use of the first Cushman version before retirement, but does not establish when either version launched.
