## What happened
On April 23, 2024, Microsoft introduced the Phi-3 family of open-weight small language models under the MIT License, launching Phi-3-mini (3.8 billion parameters) on Azure AI Studio, Hugging Face, and Ollama alongside its arXiv technical report. Trained on 3.3 trillion tokens of filtered web text and synthetic data (examples generated by larger models to teach reasoning), Phi-3-mini shipped in 4K- and 128K-token context variants small enough to run locally on smartphones. Microsoft also detailed upcoming 7B Phi-3-small and 14B Phi-3-medium models trained on 4.8 trillion tokens, reporting Phi-3-mini scores comparable to larger models such as GPT-3.5 and Mixtral 8x7B.

## Why it matters
Phi-3 demonstrated that high-quality synthetic training data can pack strong language, math, and coding abilities into compact open models built for edge and on-device deployment.
