## What happened
On June 27, 2024, Google DeepMind released Gemma 2 in 9-billion and 27-billion parameter sizes—both pretrained and instruction-tuned—under its commercial-use Gemma license, followed on July 31, 2024 by a 2-billion parameter model alongside the technical report, ShieldGemma safety classifiers, and Gemma Scope interpretability tools. The decoder-only transformer architecture alternates local 4,096-token sliding-window attention with 8,192-token global attention and uses grouped-query attention (sharing key-value heads across query heads to cut memory bandwidth). The 27B model was trained from scratch on 13 trillion primarily English tokens, while the 9B (8 trillion tokens) and 2B (2 trillion tokens) models were trained with knowledge distillation—learning from a larger teacher model's output probabilities rather than raw next-token targets alone.

## Why it matters
Gemma 2 showed that knowledge distillation and memory-efficient attention could let compact open weights—including a 27B model running on a single A100 or H100 GPU—compete with much larger models.
