## What happened
On December 19, 2025, Governor Kathy Hochul signed the Responsible AI Safety and Education (RAISE) Act (S6953B/A6453B) with agreed amendments. Large developers of frontier models, the most powerful AI systems, must write and publish information about their safety protocols and report safety incidents to the state within 72 hours of determining that one occurred. The law creates an oversight office inside the Department of Financial Services that assesses these developers and issues yearly reports, and lets the Attorney General sue developers that fail to report or make false statements.

## Why it matters
New York joined California in writing rules aimed specifically at the companies building the largest AI models. The governor described the law as building on California's framework, so that two leading tech states share one standard while no federal rule exists.
