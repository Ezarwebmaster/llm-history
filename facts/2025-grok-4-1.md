## What happened
On November 17, 2025, xAI announced Grok 4.1, an update to the Grok 4 family, rolling out to all users on grok.com, X, and the iOS and Android apps in Auto mode and as a selectable model. xAI said it had tested preliminary builds in a silent rollout from November 1 to 14, and described gains in understanding tone, creativity, and hallucination reduction.

## Why it matters
Grok 4.1 showed xAI iterating quickly after Grok 4, focusing on personality and tone-awareness alongside raw reasoning as differentiators in consumer chatbots.
