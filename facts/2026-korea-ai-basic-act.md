## What happened
South Korea's Basic Act on the Development of Artificial Intelligence and Creation of a Foundation for Trust took effect on 22 January 2026. The Ministry of Science and ICT said it would grant operators a grace period of at least one year and open a support desk. The law covers the whole AI sector, not only AI features, and requires prior notice and watermarks where generative or high-impact AI is used.

## Why it matters
A single statute now governs an entire national AI economy. Its transparency and watermarking rules shape how developers ship generative products, and governments elsewhere are watching it as a template.
