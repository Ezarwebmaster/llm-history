## What happened
In February 2003, Yoshua Bengio and colleagues published a neural language model that learned numerical representations of words and used them to predict word sequences. Their tests on two text collections improved on older systems that counted short word sequences.

## Why it matters
The paper showed how learning relationships between words could help a language model generalize beyond phrases it had seen before.
