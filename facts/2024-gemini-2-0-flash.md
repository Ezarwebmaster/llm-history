## What happened
On December 11, 2024, Google DeepMind introduced Gemini 2.0 Flash as an experimental model via the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and Vertex AI, and in the Gemini web app, alongside a Multimodal Live API for real-time audio and video streaming. Google reported that Gemini 2.0 Flash outperformed Gemini 1.5 Pro on key benchmarks at twice the speed while supporting a 1-million-token context window. Beyond multimodal inputs, it added native tool calling—for Google Search, code execution, and custom functions—plus early-access native image generation and steerable multilingual text-to-speech audio marked with invisible SynthID watermarks. On February 5, 2025, Google made an updated Gemini 2.0 Flash generally available for text output in Google AI Studio and Vertex AI.

## Why it matters
Combining low-latency inference with built-in tool calling and multimodal generation turned Google's high-volume Flash tier into a foundation for interactive and multi-step software agents.
