## What happened
On February 18, 2026, Google DeepMind launched Lyria 3 in beta in the Gemini app and expanded it to YouTube's Dream Track outside the United States. Trained on captioned audio on Google's Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) with JAX and ML Pathways, the model uses latent diffusion (iteratively denoising compressed audio representations over time) to synthesize 30-second tracks with vocals and auto-generated lyrics from text, photo, or video prompts. In the Gemini app, it launched for users aged 18 and older across eight languages (English, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, and Portuguese) with Nano Banana cover art. Every track embeds SynthID, an imperceptible digital watermark, alongside in-app audio verification for checking whether uploaded audio was created with Google AI.

## Why it matters
Pairing multimodal prompt-to-song generation with built-in audio watermarking and verification brought conversational music creation and provenance checking into Google's consumer assistant.
