## What happened
On November 4, 2026, OpenAI released Images 2.6 in ChatGPT and Codex and made `gpt-image-2.6-sunburst` and `gpt-image-2.6-flare` available in the API. Flare is the smaller, speed-oriented model; Sunburst targets more detailed compositions and precise edits. Both generate and edit images.

## Why it matters
Like Images 2.5, the release offers speed and precision variants. OpenAI says 2.6 improves editing consistency and detail preservation and generates images faster. It adds GIF generation, API image markup to target edits, SVG output in the API, and a complexity setting that controls how much the model plans before rendering.
