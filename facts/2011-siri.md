## What happened
On October 4, 2011, Apple announced the iPhone 4S with Siri, an assistant people talk to in plain language. Apple says it understands context, so asking "Will I need an umbrella this weekend?" brings a weather forecast, and it can use personal data the owner allows, such as the address book. It places calls, sends messages and email, schedules meetings and reminders, and searches the internet. It shipped as a beta in English, French and German when the phone went on sale on October 14.

## Why it matters
Siri put a conversational assistant in millions of pockets and made talking to software a mass habit, years before text chatbots arrived.
