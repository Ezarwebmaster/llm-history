## What happened
On January 8, 2026, AI21 Labs released the Jamba 2 family of open-weight models under the Apache 2.0 license. Both models use AI21's hybrid Mamba-Transformer architecture, which interleaves state-space layers (a sequence layer whose memory cost stays flat as text grows) with standard Transformer attention layers over a 256,000-token context window. Jamba2 Mini is a mixture-of-experts model with 52 billion total parameters and 12 billion active per token, tuned for enterprise question answering and instruction following over long documents, while Jamba2 3B is a compact 3-billion-parameter dense model built to run locally on phones and laptops.

## Why it matters
Jamba 2 continued the push for hybrid state-space and attention architectures in open-weight enterprise models, offering long-context processing without the quadratic memory growth of pure attention networks.
