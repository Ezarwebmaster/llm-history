## What happened
OpenAI released GPT-5.2 on December 11, 2025. Instant, Thinking and Pro began rolling out in ChatGPT to paid plans, while API developers received `gpt-5.2-chat-latest`, `gpt-5.2` and `gpt-5.2-pro`. OpenAI reported improvements in coding, image understanding, long documents and multistep tool use. On its launch tests, Thinking scored 80.0% on SWE-bench Verified, versus 76.3% for GPT-5.1 Thinking.

## Why it matters
OpenAI explicitly named GPT-5.2 the new flagship in the GPT-5 family. It updated the main model series and its capabilities across tasks, rather than simply exposing GPT-5 pro through another interface. The release also added an extra-high reasoning setting and context compaction, which shrinks conversation history so agents can keep working over longer interactions.
