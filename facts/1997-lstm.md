## What happened
Published in Neural Computation, Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) gives recurrent neural networks (networks that read sequences one step at a time) a memory cell with input and output gates that open and close to keep error signals from fading away during training.

## Why it matters
Standard recurrent networks forgot earlier inputs as training signals decayed over long sequences. LSTM solved this problem and became the backbone of speech recognition, handwriting recognition, and machine translation for two decades before the Transformer.
