## What happened
OpenAI announced that GPT-5.1 Pro was beginning its rollout to all ChatGPT Pro subscribers through the model picker. It updated GPT-5 Pro; OpenAI reported that early testers preferred the newer model for writing, data science and business questions, particularly the clarity and organization of its answers.

## Why it matters
This was a separate availability milestone from GPT-5.1 Instant and Thinking, announced on November 12. Dating Pro to November 19 makes clear when subscribers could begin selecting that variant, rather than assigning every member of the family the initial launch date.
