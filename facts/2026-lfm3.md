## What happened
On October 27, 2026, Liquid AI announced LFM3, the third generation of its Liquid Foundation Models. Six models of about 2 billion parameters each — text, vision, audio, omni, and two decision models — branch from one LFM3-1.7B base pre-trained on 22 trillion tokens. The backbone mixes three layer types: gated short convolutions, sliding-window attention (attention that looks only at a fixed recent window), and global attention. The text model has a 256K-token context window and fits in about 1 GB at 4-bit precision, so it runs on phones and laptops, and every model can be fine-tuned on a single GPU. The weights are published openly on Hugging Face.

## Why it matters
Small open models that run locally let people use AI on their own devices without sending their data to a cloud, and each one can be fine-tuned into an expert for a single narrow task.
