## What happened
On 4 November 2026 Tencent announced Hy4, its latest large language model and the full release following the Hy4 preview of August 2026. Tencent says Hy4 adds visual understanding and, for the first time, computer use (operating software through a command line or an on-screen interface), with gains in following instructions, understanding long documents and carrying out long, multi-step agent tasks. Tencent says it plans to publish the model weights and a system card in the coming days. Hy4 is available through Tencent Cloud, OpenRouter and Tencent's WorkBuddy and CodeBuddy products.

## Why it matters
The step from preview to full release shows a large lab adding vision and the ability to operate software to a flagship language model, and promising open weights so others can study and run it.
