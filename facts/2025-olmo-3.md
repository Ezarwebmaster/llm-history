## What happened
On November 20, 2025, the Allen Institute for AI released Olmo 3, a family of dense language models at 7 billion and 32 billion parameters. The release includes Base, Think, Instruct and RL Zero variants, and Ai2 publishes the Dolma 3 training data, the training code and intermediate checkpoints as well as the final weights. Ai2 calls Olmo 3-Think 32B the strongest fully open 32B-scale thinking model it is aware of.

## Why it matters
Most "open" models release only their final weights. Olmo 3 publishes the whole training pipeline, so researchers can trace a model's behaviour back to its data and check how each training stage changed it.
