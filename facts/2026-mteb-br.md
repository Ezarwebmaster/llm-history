## What happened
Tardelli Ronan Coelho Stekel introduced MTEB-BR, a benchmark of 22 Brazilian Portuguese tasks across seven categories, including search, classification and similarity. It uses data created or found in Portuguese instead of translated datasets. The paper evaluates 93 embedding models.

## Why it matters
A language-specific evaluation can reveal differences hidden by a global multilingual leaderboard, helping practitioners assess how well models handle Portuguese data.
