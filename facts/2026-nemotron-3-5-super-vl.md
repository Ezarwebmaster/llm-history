## What happened
NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL on Oct. 15, 2026, alongside two smaller Bolt models for local machines. NVIDIA says the 120-billion-parameter model is about five times faster than others in its class and is built for agents that follow instructions, use tools, and read web pages, PDFs, charts and tables. It describes Super VL as comparable to its largest reasoning model at roughly one-fifth the parameter count; those are NVIDIA's own claims. The models are open and can be post-trained on private data.

## Why it matters
Open, fast vision-language models let companies run agents on their own hardware instead of sending every task to a closed API.
