## What happened
On April 8, 2026, Meta Superintelligence Labs introduced Muse Spark, which Meta calls a natively multimodal reasoning model: it handles images with text and can use tools. It was available that day at meta.ai and in the Meta AI app, with a private API preview for select users. Meta says it reaches the capabilities of its earlier Llama 4 Maverick with over ten times less training compute, and admits gaps in coding and long multi-step agent tasks.

## Why it matters
It is the first model of a new Meta family, released first inside Meta's own assistant rather than as downloadable weights.
