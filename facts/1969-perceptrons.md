## What happened
Marvin Minsky and Seymour Papert publish Perceptrons: An Introduction to Computational Geometry, with mathematical proofs of what simple perceptrons cannot compute. The XOR function is among the limits the book discusses.

## Why it matters
The book is said to have pushed AI research away from neural networks, a controversy that is still debated.
