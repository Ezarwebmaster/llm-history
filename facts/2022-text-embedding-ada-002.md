## What happened
OpenAI introduced text-embedding-ada-002, a model that produces numerical representations of text for comparison. It replaced five separate models used for text search, text similarity and code search.

## Why it matters
Developers could use one embedding model across tasks that had previously required distinct model families, simplifying the choice of model for search and similarity applications.
