## What happened
OpenAI released dated updates to GPT-Audio mini, GPT-Realtime mini, GPT-4o mini Transcribe and GPT-4o mini TTS. It reported fewer invented words during silence or background noise and more consistent spoken output across the models that handle those tasks.

## Why it matters
The update brought improvements to existing audio interfaces. GPT-Audio mini kept its speech-to-speech role in Chat Completions, while the updated Realtime mini served low-delay voice sessions and the transcription and speech-generation models continued to handle one direction each.
