## What happened
On 5 October 2026, Reka released a research preview of Rho-1, which it calls a 19-billion-parameter "omni-reasoning" model trained from scratch. Reka says the model understands and generates text, images and video, reasons over them, and takes actions inside a single neural network, instead of handing work between separate specialised models. It presents Rho-1 as a multimodal assistant, as an interactive simulation that can be steered while it runs, and as a controller for robots.

## Why it matters
Most agent systems at the time chained a language model to separate image, video and action models, and every handoff added delay. Reka argues that putting all of those outputs in one model removes those seams; the preview is an early public test of that idea, not a finished product.
