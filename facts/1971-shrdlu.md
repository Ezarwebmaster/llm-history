## What happened
In 1971, Terry Winograd at the MIT Artificial Intelligence Laboratory completed SHRDLU, described in his technical report "Procedures as a Representation for Data in a Computer Program for Understanding Natural Language." SHRDLU let users type English commands to move blocks, pyramids, and boxes in a simulated world, answer questions, and explain its own actions.

## Why it matters
SHRDLU was the most convincing demonstration of language understanding of its era, showing that a program could combine word meaning, context and reasoning in a small closed world.
