## What happened
On February 5, 2025, Google released Gemini 2.0 Pro Experimental (`gemini-2.0-pro-exp-02-05`) in Google AI Studio, Vertex AI, and the Gemini app for Gemini Advanced subscribers, alongside the general availability of Gemini 2.0 Flash and the public preview of Gemini 2.0 Flash-Lite. Building on earlier checkpoints such as `Gemini-Exp-1206`, it introduced a 2-million-token context window for analyzing large codebases and long documents, multimodal input with text output, and native tool calling for Google Search and code execution. Google described it as its strongest model to date for coding performance, complex prompts, mathematics, factuality, and world-knowledge reasoning.

## Why it matters
Gemini 2.0 Pro Experimental brought Google's 2-million-token context window and built-in tool calling to the Gemini 2.0 Pro tier ahead of the Gemini 2.5 Pro release.
