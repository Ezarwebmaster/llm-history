## What happened
Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30, 2026, replacing Claude Sonnet 4.6 as its mid-size model. Its documentation gives a context window of one million tokens, a maximum output of 128,000 tokens, and training data running to January 2026. Anthropic calls it the most agentic Sonnet model yet, able to plan, use tools such as browsers and terminals, and run on its own at a level that previously needed larger models; it says Sonnet 5's performance comes close to Claude Opus 4.8's on agentic tasks, in some cases matching it, while costing less to run.

## Why it matters
Anthropic's mid-tier model closed much of the gap with its flagship, at a fraction of the price.
