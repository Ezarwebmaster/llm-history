## What happened
On April 21, 2026, OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Images 2.0 on all ChatGPT plans. Its image thinking mode, available on paid plans, could plan and refine images before generation and use web search. The same day, OpenAI released `gpt-image-2` for developers through the Image API, with flexible output sizes and support for preserving fine details from reference images.

## Why it matters
OpenAI reported better instruction following and stronger rendering of complex details such as dense text. Those improvements help with designs like posters or diagrams, where the words and layout matter alongside the image itself.
