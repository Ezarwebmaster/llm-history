## What happened
OpenAI released o3-mini in ChatGPT and began rolling it out in the API on January 31, 2025. It was the first reasoning model that free ChatGPT users could try. API developers could choose low, medium, or high reasoning effort to control how much work the model did before answering. The model focused on math, science, and coding and accepted text inputs only.

## Why it matters
Developers could use lower effort when speed mattered and reserve higher effort for hard problems. OpenAI reported that medium effort matched o1 on its 2024 competition math and Codeforces programming tests. The full o3 model arrived on April 16 with the ability to reason about images and combine ChatGPT tools.
