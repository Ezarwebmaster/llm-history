## What happened
At DevDay on October 6, 2025, OpenAI released GPT Image 1 mini for image generation and editing. OpenAI describes it as a cost-efficient version of GPT Image 1 that takes text and images as input and produces images.

## Why it matters
The smaller option gave developers another choice for applications that need to create or edit images with a lower operating cost.
