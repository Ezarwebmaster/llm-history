## What happened
Researchers submitted ALBERT, a paper on training language representations with fewer parameters, the learned values in a neural network. It proposed two parameter-reduction techniques and a training task that considers coherence between sentences.

## Why it matters
The authors reported that their best model achieved strong results on language-understanding benchmarks with fewer parameters than BERT-large. The work explored how to scale language models while reducing memory use, rather than simply increasing their size.
