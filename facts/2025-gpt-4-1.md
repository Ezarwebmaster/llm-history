## What happened
OpenAI released GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini and GPT-4.1 nano in its API. All three could handle up to 1 million tokens, about eight times GPT-4 Turbo's 128,000-token window. OpenAI reported that GPT-4.1 made fewer unrelated code changes and followed patch formats more reliably than GPT-4o. In OpenAI's SWE-bench Verified evaluation, where models produce patches for software issues, GPT-4.1 solved 54.6% of the tasks evaluated, compared with 33.2% for GPT-4o.

## Why it matters
A developer could provide more of a codebase alongside instructions, tests and documentation. More reliable patches made it easier to review and apply a targeted change while preserving surrounding code.
