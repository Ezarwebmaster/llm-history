## What happened
OpenAI released o4-mini alongside o3, optimizing the smaller model for fast, efficient reasoning, especially in mathematics, coding and visual tasks. Like o3, it could choose and use tools in ChatGPT while working on a problem. Free ChatGPT users could try it through the Think option.

## Why it matters
Compared with o3, o4-mini supported higher usage limits, making reasoning with tools more practical for frequent use. OpenAI reported strong mathematics results when it had a Python interpreter, a condition that makes direct comparisons with models without tools misleading.
