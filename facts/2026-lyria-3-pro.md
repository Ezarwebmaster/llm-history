## What happened
On March 25, 2026, Google DeepMind introduced Lyria 3 Pro, an advanced music generation model expanding on the Lyria 3 family. Unlike standard 30-second clip generators, Lyria 3 Pro creates complete songs up to 3 minutes long with structural awareness, enabling creators to prompt for explicit components including intros, verses, choruses, and bridges. It accepts multimodal inputs, allowing reference images or videos to guide mood and atmosphere alongside timed lyrics or purely instrumental arrangements. DeepMind launched it across Vertex AI in public preview, Google AI Studio and the Gemini API (`lyria-3-pro-preview`), Google Vids, the Gemini app for paid subscribers, and ProducerAI. All outputs incorporate Google's SynthID watermark and C2PA Content Credentials.

## Why it matters
Extending music generation from short audio clips to 3-minute structured songs with explicit section control brought long-form musical composition into enterprise creative pipelines.
