## What happened
OpenAI made the full o1 model available in ChatGPT on December 5, following the September release of o1-preview. OpenAI trained the o1 series with reinforcement learning, which rewards behaviors that lead to better answers. The models can use a sequence of internal steps to try different approaches and catch mistakes before replying.

## Why it matters
The o1 series introduced inference-time reasoning to OpenAI’s lineup. Inference is when a trained model runs to answer a request. OpenAI reported that giving o1 more time to work through a problem at that stage improved its results, making extra computation per answer another way to improve performance.
