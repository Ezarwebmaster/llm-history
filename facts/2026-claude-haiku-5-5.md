## What happened
On October 7, 2026, Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5, the small model in its Claude 5.5 family. Anthropic says it is the cheapest, fastest and most capable small model it has released. It is the first Haiku-class model with an adjustable effort setting, which lets users choose between lower cost and higher quality. Anthropic says it handles quick, repetitive jobs such as summaries, classification and database queries, and can serve as a helper model under a larger Claude model.

## Why it matters
Small, cheap models make it practical to run many routine AI tasks that were too costly before. Anthropic says Haiku 5.5 is not meant for complex agentic coding, where its larger models remain the better choice.
