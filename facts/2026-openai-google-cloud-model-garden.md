## What happened
On October 21, 2026, OpenAI and Google Cloud announced a partnership to bring OpenAI's models and Codex agent workflows into Google Cloud Model Garden, with purchases through Google Cloud Marketplace. OpenAI said general availability was planned for the coming weeks.

## Why it matters
The partnership gives organizations another way to use OpenAI's models inside a cloud environment where they already manage data and software.
