## What happened
On February 26, 2026, Google DeepMind launched Nano Banana 2 (Gemini 3.1 Flash Image), a native multimodal image generation and editing model built on Gemini 3 Flash. Rolled out across the Gemini app, Search AI Mode and Lens, Flow, Google Ads, Workspace, Google Antigravity, and in preview via Google AI Studio and Vertex AI, it accepts up to 1 million input tokens across text, images, audio, and video to output text and images from 512px up to 4K resolution across aspect ratios up to 8:1 and 1:8. It adds configurable thinking levels, web and image-search grounding, in-image text localization, consistency across five characters and 14 objects, and SynthID plus C2PA provenance, reaching 1079 Elo on GenAI-Bench overall preference.

## Why it matters
Pairing multimodal reasoning, live search grounding, and 4K visual fidelity with Flash speed brought studio-grade generation and iterative editing to everyday and high-volume workflows.
