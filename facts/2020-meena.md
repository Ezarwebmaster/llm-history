## What happened
On January 28, 2020, Google Research introduced Meena, an end-to-end neural conversational model with 2.6 billion parameters. It used an Evolved Transformer encoder and decoder and was trained on 341 GB of public social-media conversation text. Google proposed Sensibleness and Specificity Average, a human-evaluation measure for whether replies make sense and fit the conversation.

## Why it matters
Meena made open-domain dialogue a measurable language-model problem: responses could be judged for both being sensible and being specific, rather than merely fluent.
