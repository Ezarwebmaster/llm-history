## What happened
On October 28, 2026, NVIDIA published Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, a speech model that transcribes recordings of up to one hour in 40 languages, either verbatim or in written form. A plain-language instruction chooses the style for each request. The model can also label speakers, add word timestamps and translate speech. NVIDIA releases the weights under the OpenMDW-1.1 license and says it is the best open-weights model on the English and multilingual Open ASR Leaderboards.

## Why it matters
Transcription, speaker labels and translation in one open model let developers run speech tasks on their own hardware instead of through a closed service.
