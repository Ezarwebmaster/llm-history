## What happened
In a paper first submitted on February 25, 2017, Baidu researchers described Deep Voice, a text-to-speech system made from neural networks for tasks such as pronunciation, timing, pitch and audio generation. They reported optimized CPU inference at real-time or faster speeds for some model sizes.

## Why it matters
The work showed that neural networks could handle the separate stages of speech synthesis with less hand-built feature engineering, while producing speech quickly enough for live use.
