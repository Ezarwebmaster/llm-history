## What happened
On November 2, 2026, Deep Principle introduced Hydrogen-1.0, its first general-purpose large model, built by post-training Z.ai's GLM-5.3 on data from chemistry, materials science and life sciences, plus records and agent trajectories from its own laboratories. The company published open weights on Hugging Face and ModelScope and made the model available in its MIRA AI Scientist product. Deep Principle reports 38.3% on FrontierScience Research, five points above GPT-6 Astra, and 93.0% on GPQA Diamond, close to Kimi K3's 93.5%.

## Why it matters
It is an open-weight attempt to specialise a general model for scientific discovery, from a lab new to the timeline, so researchers can inspect and adapt the weights themselves.
