## What happened
On January 7, 1954, in New York, an IBM 701 computer translated more than sixty Russian sentences into English. Linguists from Georgetown University submitted brief statements in Russian on politics, law, mathematics, chemistry and other subjects. A young woman who did not understand Russian punched them onto IBM cards. The 701 printed English translations within a few seconds, and IBM's press release says all of the more than sixty sentences translated smoothly.

## Why it matters
The demonstration was meant to win government and public interest, and funding, for machine translation. It showed that a computer could convert meaning from one language to another.
