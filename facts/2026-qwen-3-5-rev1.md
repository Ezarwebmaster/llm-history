## What happened
On 16 February 2026, the Qwen team released the first model in its Qwen3.5 series, Qwen3.5-397B-A17B, with downloadable weights under the Apache 2.0 licence. It uses a mixture of experts, a design that activates only part of the model for each piece of input: 397 billion parameters in total and 17 billion active per token. It can process both text and images.

## Why it matters
The release gives developers and researchers a model they can run and adapt themselves for tasks involving both visual and written information.
