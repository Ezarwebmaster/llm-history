## What happened
On October 15, 2026, OpenAI announced GPT-Live-1.1 was rolling out to ChatGPT, Codex and Dots users, with 13 new voices and better performance in several non-English languages. OpenAI also reported more natural delegation and improved steerability. Its API changelog listed GPT-Live-1.1 on November 19, with improvements to pronunciation, accent consistency and transcription.

## Why it matters
Like GPT-Live-1, the successor can listen while speaking and delegate deeper work to a backend. OpenAI describes the API model as improving multilingual speech, instruction following and voice fidelity, making its continuous voice interface more usable across languages.
