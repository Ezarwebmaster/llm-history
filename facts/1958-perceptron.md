## What happened
In the November 1958 issue of Psychological Review, Frank Rosenblatt of the Cornell Aeronautical Laboratory published a theory of a hypothetical nervous system he called a perceptron. The paper asks how a system senses the physical world, how it remembers what it senses, and how that memory shapes its behavior. Rosenblatt writes that the theory lets one predict learning curves.

## Why it matters
The perceptron is an early example of a machine that learns from examples instead of following hand-written rules, an idea that later neural networks, including language models, build on.
