## What happened
Meituan releases LongCat-2.5 on November 2, 2026. The model has 1.6 trillion total parameters with about 48 billion activated per token. It handles text, images and video, and is trained on Chinese-made AI chips. Meituan reports it scores 88.4 on Terminal Bench v2.1 for coding agents.

## Why it matters
LongCat-2.5 shows that a Chinese company can train a frontier-scale model on domestic hardware, and it targets the long-running agent tasks — working inside large codebases, building full projects — that labs are racing to solve.
