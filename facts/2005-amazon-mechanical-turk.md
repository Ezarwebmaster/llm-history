## What happened
On November 2, 2005, Amazon announced Amazon Mechanical Turk, a web services API that lets a computer program ask a human being to perform a task and get the results back, and can coordinate many people at once. AWS opened the service in beta that day. In the announcement, Amazon argues that people still outperform computers at simple jobs such as identifying objects in photographs, and calls the service a way to integrate "Artificial Artificial Intelligence" into software.

## Why it matters
Mechanical Turk turned human labour into a web service that programs could call. It became the standard way to gather labels, ratings and other human-written data, and later the model of human feedback and human-in-the-loop pipelines used to train and evaluate AI systems.
