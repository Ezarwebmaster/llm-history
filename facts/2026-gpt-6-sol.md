## What happened
OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol alongside GPT-6 Luna in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API. OpenAI described Sol as bringing some of the GPT-6 Astra family's capabilities to a model suited to repeated work across coding, computer use and professional tasks.

## Why it matters
The release offered a different balance of capability and operating cost within the GPT-6 family, widening access to advanced reasoning for ongoing workflows.
