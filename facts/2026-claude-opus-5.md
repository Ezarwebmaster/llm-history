## What happened
Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 on July 24, 2026. Its documentation gives a context window of one million tokens, a maximum output of 128,000 tokens, and training data running to May 2026. Callers set an effort level, which tells the model how much to think before answering. Anthropic says Opus 5 comes close to Claude Fable 5 on its own evaluations at a lower cost per task, that it more than doubles Claude Opus 4.8's result on Frontier-Bench v0.1, and that on ARC-AGI 3, a test of puzzle-solving, its score is three times the next-best model's. Anthropic also says the model still shows important limitations on long-running, autonomous research.

## Why it matters
Anthropic's pitch was cost rather than a new ceiling: near-frontier work for jobs that run for hours.
