## What happened
Moonshot introduces Kimi K3.1 Pro, the first model in its K3.1 series, a mixture-of-experts model that routes each piece of input through part of its parameters. Moonshot reports 65.7% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, a coding benchmark, and says full weights will follow by Nov. 4.

## Why it matters
It gave developers a strong open-weight coding model from a lab that had been absent from the open-model race.
