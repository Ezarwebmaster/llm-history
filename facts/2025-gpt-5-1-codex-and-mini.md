## What happened
OpenAI released both models in the Responses API on November 13, 2025. GPT-5.1-Codex adapted the general-purpose GPT-5.1 for coding agents in Codex and similar environments. OpenAI describes Mini as a smaller, less capable and more economical version of Codex. Both are distinct from GPT-5.1-Codex-Max, announced November 19 for longer-running work across entire projects.

## Why it matters
The pair gave developers a choice between a coding-specialized model and a smaller variant, instead of relying solely on a general-purpose model. The later Max model targeted long tasks with a different design for working across multiple context windows.
