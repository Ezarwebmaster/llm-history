## What happened
OpenAI described limited API access to `gpt-4-32k` in its March 14 launch announcement and named `gpt-4-32k-0314` as the initial version. The window held 32,768 tokens, four times the standard GPT-4 limit of 8,192. On June 13, OpenAI announced the updated `gpt-4-32k-0613` with the same function-calling improvements as the standard June version.

## Why it matters
The larger window let developers give GPT-4 more of a long document or conversation in one request. It was an intermediate step between the original API's standard context and the larger GPT-4 Turbo window announced later that year.
