## What happened
On August 13, 2026, Google introduced Gemini 3.7 Flash across Google AI Studio, Android Studio, the Gemini API, Google Antigravity, and Gemini Enterprise. Arriving three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash, the model brought algorithmic upgrades for coding, UI generation, and multi-step agent planning. It accepts text, image, audio, video, and PDF inputs across a 1M-token context window with 64K output tokens, introducing configurable thinking levels (low, medium, and high). Google reported 65.3% on DeepSWE v1.1 (up from 49.0% on 3.6 Flash), 43.6% on FrontierCode 1.1 Main, 1588 Elo on WebDev Arena, and 34.0% on GDP.pdf.

## Why it matters
Gemini 3.7 Flash brought configurable reasoning depth and significant coding gains to Google's high-efficiency Flash tier, improving agentic planning and UI engineering.
