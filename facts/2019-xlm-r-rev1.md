## What happened
On November 5, 2019, Alexis Conneau and colleagues submitted the first version of their XLM-R paper to arXiv. It describes a Transformer-based masked language model trained on more than two terabytes of filtered Common Crawl data in 100 languages. The paper reports gains over multilingual BERT on several cross-language tests.

## Why it matters
The paper showed that training a multilingual model on more data improved transfer across languages, with particularly large reported gains for lower-resource languages such as Swahili and Urdu.
