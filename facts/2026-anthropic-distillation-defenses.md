## What happened
On October 12, 2026, Anthropic published "Strengthening our defenses against distillation attacks," which describes three measures: limits on access to reasoning transcripts, a two-stage classifier that blocks extraction attempts, and stricter usage limits for users who keep trying. Anthropic says early evidence shows the data harvested by distillers fell by about 90 to 99%, and that it has banned tens of thousands of accounts associated with distillation.

## Why it matters
Distillation means training a model on another model's outputs. Anthropic calls it an attack, and its measures show what a lab can do against it: access rules for reasoning, classifiers and account bans.
