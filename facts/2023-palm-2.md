## What happened
On May 10, 2023, at Google I/O, Google introduced PaLM 2, its next-generation language model featuring enhanced multilingual, reasoning, and coding capabilities. PaLM 2 was trained across more than 100 spoken languages and public source code datasets in languages including Python, JavaScript, Fortran, and Verilog. Built with compute-optimal scaling principles, it was developed in four model sizes: Gecko, Otter, Bison, and Unicorn, with Gecko lightweight enough to run locally on mobile devices. At launch, PaLM 2 powered more than 25 Google products and features, including an updated version of Bard, Google Workspace integrations, Med-PaLM 2, and Sec-PaLM.

## Why it matters
PaLM 2 marked Google's shift to compute-optimal scaling and modular model sizes, bringing localized on-device inference to phones and production foundation models into dozens of Google products.
