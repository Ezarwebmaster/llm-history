## What happened
OpenAI publicly announced its CSAM Image Classifier in its November 16 release notes. The service helps approved organizations review images for potential child sexual abuse material, including AI-generated material. Access through the API requires an application, verification, program onboarding and acceptance of applicable terms.

## Why it matters
The classifier supplies scores to help trained reviewers prioritize their work. OpenAI says it does not replace human judgment: organizations retain responsibility for reviewing results, deciding what action to take and meeting reporting obligations. The announcement describes restricted organizational access rather than a generally available consumer tool.
