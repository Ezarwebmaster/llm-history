## What happened
On 2 August 2026 the AI Act became applicable, two years after it entered into force. From that day the Commission's AI Office and the national authorities are responsible for enforcing it; the AI Office can demand technical documentation from general-purpose AI providers, evaluate their models, require corrective measures and fine them. The transparency rules for AI-generated content also take effect, while the rules for some high-risk systems wait until December 2027 and for products built into lifts or toys until August 2028.

## Why it matters
The first broad AI law now has teeth: providers of general-purpose models sold in Europe can be asked for documentation, examined and fined, not merely told what the law says.
