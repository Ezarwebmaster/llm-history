## What happened
On March 25, 2025, OpenAI began rolling out GPT-4o's native image generation in ChatGPT, with API access still to come. On April 23, it made the image model available to developers as GPT Image 1. It can generate and edit images from text and image inputs, including text within images.

## Why it matters
A capability built into a multimodal language model became available for developers to incorporate into their own tools, extending image generation beyond the ChatGPT interface.
