## What happened
On December 11, 2025, OpenAI began rolling out GPT-5.2 Pro in ChatGPT, starting with paid plans, and made `gpt-5.2-pro` available in the Responses API. OpenAI described Pro as the option for difficult questions where a better answer is worth waiting for; its documentation notes that some requests can take minutes.

## Why it matters
The existing GPT-5.2 launch describes the broader flagship series. Pro was one of its variants, alongside the faster Instant and deeper-reasoning Thinking, giving users another choice for demanding work rather than a new numbered generation.
