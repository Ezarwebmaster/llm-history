## What happened
On March 31, 2026, Google DeepMind launched Veo 3.1 Lite on the paid tier of the Gemini API and Google AI Studio, followed by a Google Cloud Preview on April 2 and a model card on April 8. Google says it matches Veo 3.1 Fast in speed at less than half the cost. Supporting text-to-video and image-to-video prompts, it outputs 4-, 6-, or 8-second clips with audio at 24 fps in 720p or 1080p (16:9 or 9:16), plus video extension and Content Credentials (C2PA) provenance metadata. In human evaluations against Veo 3.1 Fast, it achieved a 54.6% text-to-video win rate (1,000 prompts) and 47.2% image-to-video win rate (646 prompts). Its Gemini API preview shut down on October 22, 2026, while Cloud Preview remains active.

## Why it matters
Bringing native audio, 1080p video, and Fast-tier speed to a lighter tier made video generation practical for high-volume iteration.
