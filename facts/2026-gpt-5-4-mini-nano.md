## What happened
OpenAI jointly released GPT-5.4 mini and nano through the Responses and Chat Completions APIs. Mini also reached Codex and ChatGPT. OpenAI positioned mini for responsive coding, tool use and interpreting images, including computer interfaces; nano targeted classification, extraction, ranking and simpler coding support. The API changelog distinguishes mini's built-in computer use and tool search from nano, which lacks those tools.

## Why it matters
The smaller models extended GPT-5.4 to workloads that repeat many narrow tasks. OpenAI described larger models handling planning and final judgment while mini subagents performed supporting work in parallel. Nano offered a still smaller option for simpler tasks where response time and resource use mattered most. This made the family useful beyond its flagship's more demanding assignments.
