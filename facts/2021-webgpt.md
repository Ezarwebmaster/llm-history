## What happened
OpenAI trained GPT-3 to search, navigate pages and collect passages using a text-based browser before writing answers with citations. It learned from human demonstrations and a model trained to predict human preferences.

## Why it matters
WebGPT demonstrated a way to ground open-ended answers in retrieved evidence and make their factual claims easier for people to check. OpenAI noted that unfamiliar questions and unreliable sources remained challenges.
