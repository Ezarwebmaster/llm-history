## What happened
On October 27, 2026, Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, a world model: a system that generates and predicts how a scene unfolds. Users steer a running scene in real time with natural language, for example by moving the camera, changing the weather or adding characters, and every scene produces sound on the fly. Google says it has started rolling Genie 4 out to a limited group of trusted testers, and that it is coming soon to Google AI Ultra subscribers and to early-access partners in the Gemini API and AI Studio.

## Why it matters
Google says such simulated worlds can give robots and AI agents safe places to train before they act in the real world. Google calls this a crucial step toward artificial general intelligence, which is the company's own claim.
