## What happened
On June 23, 2026, ByteDance's Seed team released the Seed 2.1 family in two sizes, Pro and Turbo. The models are built for agents that finish jobs: they plan and carry out multi-step office work, moving between a search engine, a browser, code repositories and files, and they write, debug and validate software end to end. ByteDance says Seed 2.1 Pro scores highest on GDPVal, a benchmark of real work products, sits near the top of the Agents' Last Exam, and takes the top score on the MobileWorld benchmark of phone-screen tasks. They are served through ByteDance's own BytePlus playground and API.

## Why it matters
ByteDance serves these models through its own API rather than open weights, and its bet is on agents that finish real work in one run: slides, spreadsheets, reports, and code that reaches a debugged and tested state.
