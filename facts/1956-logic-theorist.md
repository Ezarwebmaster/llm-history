## What happened
Allen Newell, Herbert Simon and Cliff Shaw finished Logic Theorist in 1956. The program searched for proofs of theorems in Whitehead and Russell's Principia Mathematica, proving 38 of the first 52 and finding a shorter proof of Theorem 2.85. It was hand-simulated first, then run on a computer at RAND.

## Why it matters
Logic Theorist showed that a machine could handle non-numerical reasoning, not just arithmetic. Its ideas — search trees trimmed by heuristics and list-based symbol processing — shaped later AI programs and the Lisp language.
