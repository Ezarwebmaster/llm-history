## What happened
On December 1, 2025, DeepSeek launched DeepSeek-V3.2 as the official successor to V3.2-Exp, live on its app, web and API, alongside DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale, an API-only variant for evaluating maximum reasoning. DeepSeek calls both reasoning-first models built for agents, and says V3.2 is the first DeepSeek model to integrate thinking, the step-by-step hidden reasoning, directly into tool use, in both thinking and non-thinking modes. V3.2-Speciale reached gold-level results in the 2025 IMO, CMO, ICPC World Finals and IOI, in DeepSeek's report. Both models' weights were posted on Hugging Face.

## Why it matters
A frontier open-weight model that thinks while it calls tools made agent work something developers could run and inspect, not only rent from a closed API.
