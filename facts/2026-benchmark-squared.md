## What happened
A research paper proposed three measures for evaluating benchmarks: agreement with rankings from other benchmarks, ability to distinguish models, and cases where weaker models succeed on questions that stronger ones fail. The authors tested the framework across 15 benchmarks and 11 language models.

## Why it matters
A benchmark's score is useful only if its questions measure capabilities reliably. The proposed measures offer ways to examine the tests themselves rather than treating every leaderboard as equally informative.
