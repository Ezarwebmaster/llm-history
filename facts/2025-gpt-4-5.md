## What happened
OpenAI released GPT-4.5 as a research preview on February 27, 2025, initially to ChatGPT Pro users and developers on paid API tiers. OpenAI reported more natural conversation and fewer hallucinations from scaling pretraining, the learning phase before task-specific tuning. Unlike o1, GPT-4.5 did not deliberate before replying.

The API preview shut down on July 14, 2025, with GPT-4.1 listed as its replacement. That was not its ChatGPT retirement: OpenAI's May 28, 2026 notes scheduled removal from ChatGPT for June 27, 2026, explicitly separating the product change from the API.

## Why it matters
GPT-4.5 represented OpenAI's parallel investment in broad knowledge and conversational quality while its o-series developed longer reasoning. Its different API and ChatGPT lifetimes also show why a model's retirement needs a product-specific date.
