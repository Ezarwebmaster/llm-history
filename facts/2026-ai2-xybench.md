## What happened
Ai2 and collaborators released XYBench, a benchmark of 8,115 questions that asks whether assistants recognize a misconception and steer toward the user's real goal. The questions cover programming and everyday activities.

## Why it matters
Users may ask how to implement a solution that will not solve their actual problem. In Ai2's tests, the five evaluated models often stayed focused on the mistaken approach. The benchmark separates identifying the misconception from emphasizing a useful alternative, making those behaviors easier to study.
