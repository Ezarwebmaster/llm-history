## What happened
Ai2 released FairStress, a benchmark with decision scenarios in areas including hiring and healthcare. It varies demographic details and challenges model choices with matched persuasive arguments. Ai2 reported that models could reach the same choice across scenarios yet differ in how easily they changed it.

## Why it matters
A final answer alone can conceal differences in how consistently a model applies evidence. The benchmark tests an equality-centered definition of fairness; it is one way to examine a broader and contested question.
