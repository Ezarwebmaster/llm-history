## What happened
On July 30, Google DeepMind announced Gemini Robotics ER 2, a model that plans tasks for robots, interprets ongoing video and calls tools such as navigation or movement controls. Developers could use it through the Gemini API and AI Studio, with private preview on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Google demonstrated it directing Boston Dynamics' Spot to fetch an object.

## Why it matters
It separates a robot's high-level planning from the software that carries out its movements, while letting the planner check video and adjust a task as it unfolds.
