## What happened
On October 28, 2026, Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon, designed for cybersecurity and long-horizon engineering work. Google said it would first reach a subset of cyber defenders, then additional trusted partners through its Fairwind Program, while broader access was prepared. Google reported scores of 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for finding vulnerabilities, 54.8% on ExploitGym for exploitation, and 71.0% on CWE-bench for patching.

## Why it matters
Carbon’s staged rollout puts an advanced model’s ability to find, test, and repair software flaws at the center of its release. The initial restriction to defenders also shows how a lab can use limited access and pre-release testing before offering powerful cybersecurity tools more broadly.
