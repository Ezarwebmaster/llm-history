## What happened
On February 16, 2011, IBM's Watson computer won a televised three-day Jeopardy! match against the quiz show's two most successful human champions, Ken Jennings and Brad Rutter. Running on IBM's DeepQA architecture across 90 servers with 2,880 processor cores, Watson analyzed natural-language clues, searched local encyclopedias, dictionaries, and literary works without an internet connection, scored candidate answers, and buzzed in when confidence thresholds were met. Watson finished in first place with 77,147 points, ahead of Jennings with 24,000 and Rutter with 21,600.

## Why it matters
Watson showed that automated systems could parse puns, wordplay, and complex natural-language queries at human speed, advancing question answering from keyword retrieval into semantic reasoning.
