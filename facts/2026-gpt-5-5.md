## What happened
OpenAI began rolling out GPT-5.5 in ChatGPT and Codex, and GPT-5.5 Pro in ChatGPT. On April 24 both became available in the API: GPT-5.5 through Responses and Chat Completions, and Pro through Responses. Pro was intended for harder questions and work requiring higher accuracy, with more computation for difficult problems.

## Why it matters
OpenAI presented GPT-5.5 as a model for extended, multi-part work: writing and debugging code, research, analyzing data, creating documents and operating software. On OpenAI's internal Expert-SWE test of long-horizon coding tasks, the company reported that GPT-5.5 outperformed GPT-5.4. The release aimed to let an assistant plan, use tools and continue across steps of professional tasks.
