## What happened
On September 3, 2026, NVIDIA announced an agreement to acquire Hugging Face, the platform where labs publish model weights and datasets for anyone to download. NVIDIA gives its size as more than 18 million developers, researchers and creators, over 3 million models, 500,000 datasets, 1 million applications and more than 200,000 companies. NVIDIA says Hugging Face "will remain an open platform for the entire AI ecosystem", that it will keep supporting open-source and open-weight models from across that ecosystem, and that its own chips "will not be required to build on or deploy through Hugging Face".

## Why it matters
Almost every open-weight release in this timeline is published on Hugging Face. One chip maker owning that shared shelf makes its promises of neutrality something the rest of the field now has to take on trust.
