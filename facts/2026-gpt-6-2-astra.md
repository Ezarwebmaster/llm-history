## What happened
On November 17, 2026, OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra (`gpt-6.2-astra`) in ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the OpenAI API as its flagship model for demanding software engineering, frontend and 3D design, scientific computing, and structured professional documents. It supports a 1,050,000-token context window (up to 922,000 input tokens and 128,000 output tokens) and can format documents, spreadsheets, and slides to match supplied templates.

## Why it matters
OpenAI reports that GPT-6.2 Astra gained 6.2 percentage points over GPT-6 Astra on GDPval v2 while using 22% fewer output tokens, improved by 9 percentage points on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1, and scored 5.2 points higher on Agents' Last Exam v1.1. Its system card also notes it is the first externally released OpenAI model to meet its High threshold for AI Self-Improvement.
