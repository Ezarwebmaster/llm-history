## What happened
On October 26, 2026, OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Terra, a model it positions for high-volume professional work: drafting, visual reasoning over documents and drawings, and computer use, where an agent operates software interfaces. It is available in the OpenAI API as `gpt-6.1-terra` and in ChatGPT Work and Codex, but not in regular ChatGPT chat. OpenAI reports that at maximum reasoning effort it solves 50.3% of Terminal-Bench 4.0 coding-agent tasks, more than double GPT-5.6 Terra's published 21.5%, and that the error rate on its internal error-inducing prompts fell from 53.6% (GPT-5.6 Terra, low effort) to 16.7%.

## Why it matters
OpenAI is putting its effort into cheap, fast computer-use agents rather than only frontier reasoning: Terra is pitched as doing GPT-6 Astra-level work at a fraction of the cost, which decides what kinds of office work get automated.
