## What happened
DeepSeek released DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 on Oct. 17, 2026, as an update to V4.1 Flash, according to its API change log. It reports 42.6 on Humanity's Last Exam without tools, 42.3 on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and 77.0 on DeepSWE v1.1, and says the new version runs faster and writes shorter answers than its predecessor. The model handles text and images, and its weights are on Hugging Face under the MIT licence.

## Why it matters
The release is a small, fast model aimed at many cheap agent tasks rather than a single hard one.
