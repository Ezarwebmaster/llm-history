## What happened
On March 3, 2026, OpenAI released GPT-5.3 Instant to all ChatGPT users and to developers as `gpt-5.3-chat-latest`. OpenAI said it improved everyday conversations, web-search answers and accuracy while reducing unnecessary refusals and caveats compared with GPT-5.2 Instant. Paid users could still select GPT-5.2 Instant as a legacy model for three months.

## Why it matters
This was an update to the fast conversational option, distinct from the broader GPT-5.2 series launch. GPT-5.3-Codex, introduced on February 5, was a separate model focused on coding and long-running computer tasks.
