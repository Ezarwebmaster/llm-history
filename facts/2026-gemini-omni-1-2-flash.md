## What happened
On November 5, 2026, Google began rolling out Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash and made it generally available through the Gemini API and Gemini for business. The model accepts text, images, audio and video, then produces video with audio. It can generate clips from 3 to 15 seconds long and edit the same clip across several conversational turns, such as changing a background or lighting without starting over. Google says the release improves how consistently a clip follows reference images and keeps characters, objects and scenes stable.

## Why it matters
Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash joins conversational instructions with video generation and editing, letting people refine moving images through follow-up requests rather than rebuilding each clip.
