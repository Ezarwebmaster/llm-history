## What happened
A research preprint introduces MDU, a benchmark with 23,146 examples across 13 document tasks in English, French, German, Italian and Spanish. It evaluates models that interpret images and text together.

## Why it matters
Document benchmarks often emphasize English and narrow tasks. Testing several languages and tasks can reveal gaps that an overall score alone might hide.
