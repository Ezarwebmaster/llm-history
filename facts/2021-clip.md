## What happened
OpenAI introduced CLIP, trained on images paired with text from the internet to identify which text belongs with an image. It learns to align visual concepts with language: for example, it can compare an image with descriptions of a cat and a dog to choose a label.

## Why it matters
CLIP applies natural language supervision to vision and can classify images using category descriptions without task-specific training. OpenAI compared this flexibility to GPT-2 and GPT-3's ability to perform tasks without examples. CLIP matches images and text; it does not itself generate images.
