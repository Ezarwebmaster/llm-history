## What happened
Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 4.6 as an upgrade across coding, long-context reasoning, agent planning, and computer use. Available as the default model on claude.ai for Free and Pro users, it introduced a 1-million-token context window in beta while keeping Sonnet tier responsiveness.

## Why it matters
Sonnet 4.6 brought capabilities previously associated with frontier Opus-tier models into the faster, efficient Sonnet tier, enabling broader enterprise deployment of autonomous agents and computer navigation.
