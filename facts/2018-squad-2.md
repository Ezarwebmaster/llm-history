## What happened
On June 11, Pranav Rajpurkar, Robin Jia and Percy Liang posted their paper on SQuAD 2.0. They asked contributors to read a passage and write plausible questions that it left unanswered. The researchers mixed those questions with the answerable ones from the earlier Stanford Question Answering Dataset.

## Why it matters
On the earlier test, a reader could always pick some words from the passage. The new test rewarded leaving the answer blank when the passage lacked evidence. One system's F1 score, which measures overlap with the accepted answer, fell from 86% on the earlier test to 66% on the new one.
