## What happened
On November 12, 2025 OpenAI announced GPT-5.1, an upgrade to GPT-5. The company described GPT-5.1 Instant as warmer and better at following instructions and GPT-5.1 Thinking as adapting how long it reasons to the task, rolling out to ChatGPT users and the API.

## Why it matters
GPT-5 was the default ChatGPT model, so a 5.1 update that changes tone, instruction-following and reasoning control quickly reaches hundreds of millions of users and developers.
