## What happened
In November 1961, MIT demonstrated an experimental version of the Compatible Time-Sharing System (CTSS) on a modified IBM 709 mainframe, which let several people use it at once through terminals. In 1962 the mainframe was replaced by a modified IBM 7090, and a 1963 programmer's guide describes the system on that hardware.

## Why it matters
CTSS helped show that a single mainframe could support interactive work by many people, an approach that shaped later time-sharing systems.
