## What happened
Cai and collaborators presented a benchmark of 2,246 multiple-choice questions spanning nine reasoning types. It tests whether models use subtle contextual clues alongside explicit instructions. The authors also proposed a prompting method to recover overlooked causal relations.

## Why it matters
The study probes a failure mode in which a model focuses on a stated task while missing context needed to answer it. The authors reported low consistency across the models they evaluated.
