## What happened
Terra arrived alongside Sol and Luna in the July 9 GPT-5.6 rollout. Developers could call it as `gpt-5.6-terra` and adjust how much effort it spent reasoning. At launch, Free and Go users got Terra in ChatGPT Work and Codex, while Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users could choose among all three there.

## Why it matters
Terra gave users a middle option between Sol for the most demanding work and Luna for fast, high-volume tasks. OpenAI positioned Terra as competitive with the previous flagship GPT-5.5 and described its place in the lineup as roughly equivalent to the mini tier in earlier GPT-5 families.
