## What happened
On November 10, 2026, ElevenLabs released Scribe v3 for recorded audio and Scribe v3 Live for real-time voice agents via ElevenAPI. ElevenLabs reports that Scribe v3 cuts word errors by 27% versus Scribe v2 across 102 languages—reducing average word error rate on the FLEURS benchmark from 16.94% to 12.38%—and supports speaker diarization (labeling who spoke when) for up to 32 speakers. ElevenLabs reports Scribe v3 Live lowers semantic word error rate by 43% over Scribe v2 Realtime while adding live speaker labels, mid-sentence language switching, 10,000-term keyterm libraries, and context-based turn prediction (deciding when a speaker finishes from meaning rather than silence alone).

## Why it matters
Pairing lower multilingual transcription errors with context-aware turn prediction addresses recurring voice-agent breakdowns, including cutting off speakers during natural pauses and mishearing specialized terms.
