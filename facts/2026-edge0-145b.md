## What happened
On November 12, 2026, Edge0 released Edge0-145B, an open-weight coding and agentic model derived from Qwen3.8-Flash-Next under the Edge0 Community Model License 1.0. It uses a latent sparse Mixture-of-Experts architecture—activating only 10.12 billion of its 145 billion total parameters per token across 48 layers of 512 experts—with a 256K-token context window. Alongside quantized NVFP4 and Q3_K weights, Edge0 open-sourced its streaming inference engine, Edge0-Strata, and the Edge0 Chat desktop application. By keeping frequently used experts in GPU memory, caching others in system RAM, and streaming remaining weights dynamically from an SSD, Edge0-145B runs locally on a single 24GB consumer GPU such as an RTX 5090.

## Why it matters
Tiered expert streaming lets 100B-plus-parameter sparse models run on local consumer workstations rather than requiring multi-GPU cloud servers.
