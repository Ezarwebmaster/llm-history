## What happened
On December 18, 2025, Google DeepMind released T5Gemma 2, a family of open-weight pretrained encoder-decoder models (networks using separate stacks to read inputs and generate outputs) adapted from decoder-only Gemma 3 checkpoints via the UL2 denoising recipe across roughly two trillion tokens. Released on Hugging Face, Kaggle, and Vertex AI in three sizes—270M-270M (~370M total parameters excluding the 400M frozen SigLIP vision encoder), 1B-1B (~1.7B), and 4B-4B (~7B)—T5Gemma 2 ties word embeddings across encoder and decoder and merges decoder self-attention and cross-attention into one layer. Inheriting Gemma 3's vision encoder and alternating local-global attention, it processes text and images across 140+ languages with a 128K-token context window.

## Why it matters
T5Gemma 2 brought multimodality and 128K long-context support to compact open encoder-decoder models, showing that decoder-only checkpoints can be adapted into efficient, bidirectional architectures without training from scratch.
