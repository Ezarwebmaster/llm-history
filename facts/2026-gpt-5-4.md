## What happened
OpenAI released GPT-5.4 in Codex, in ChatGPT as GPT-5.4 Thinking, and through the Chat Completions and Responses APIs. Thinking began rolling out to Plus, Team and Pro, with administrator-enabled early access for Enterprise and Edu. GPT-5.4 Pro launched on ChatGPT Pro and Enterprise and through the Responses API. It used more computation for harder problems. On the hardest tier of FrontierMath, OpenAI reported 38.0% for Pro and 27.1% for GPT-5.4.

## Why it matters
GPT-5.4 brought the coding capabilities of GPT-5.3-Codex into a general-purpose model designed for work including spreadsheets, presentations and documents. OpenAI described it as its first general-purpose model with built-in computer use. In Codex and the API, the model could interpret screenshots, issue mouse and keyboard commands, or write code to control a browser, so assistants could work through software interfaces across applications.
