## What happened
On April 29, 2026, IBM Research released three Granite Speech 4.1 models for multilingual speech recognition and translation. The 2B model recorded a 5.33% word error rate on the OpenASR Leaderboard, according to IBM. IBM described the Plus variant as offering richer transcription features and the NAR variant as processing more audio in the same amount of time.

## Why it matters
The variants let developers choose between richer transcripts and higher throughput. The NAR model generates groups of tokens together, and IBM reported that this made better use of graphics processors than producing tokens one at a time.
