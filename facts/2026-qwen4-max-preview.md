## What happened
On November 11, 2026, Alibaba Cloud launched Qwen4-Max Preview (`qwen4-max-preview`), the first public preview from its next-generation Qwen 4 flagship family. Designed for complex programming, agentic workflows, frontend design, and office productivity, the model supports multimodal inputs across text, images, and video within a 1-million-token context window. It introduces an output budget of up to 393,216 tokens shared dynamically between deep reasoning and the final response, with adjustable reasoning effort levels (`low`, `medium`, and `xhigh` by default). The preview is offered through Alibaba Cloud's Token Plan subscription through November 25, 2026.

## Why it matters
Qwen4-Max Preview marks the initial deployment of Alibaba's Qwen 4 architecture, offering unified multimodal reasoning with unprecedented output length for long-horizon agentic task execution.
