## What happened
On July 21, 2026, Google DeepMind launched Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber alongside Gemini 3.6 Flash and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite. Built on the Gemini 3.5 Flash architecture, the model is specialized for cybersecurity workflows including vulnerability analysis, penetration testing defense, and automated security code audits across a 1-million-token input window and 64K output tokens.

## Why it matters
Specializing a fast Flash-class model for defensive cybersecurity allowed enterprise security teams to perform automated code reviews and threat investigations without the latency and cost of frontier general-purpose models.
