## What happened
Paul Werbos submitted his Harvard PhD thesis, "Beyond Regression: New Tools for Prediction and Analysis in the Behavioral Sciences," in 1974. The thesis showed how to calculate all the derivatives of a computed result with respect to every input and parameter in one backward sweep through the calculation, using the chain rule. Werbos called the method dynamic feedback. In a later account, he writes that backpropagation was first spelled out explicitly and implemented in this 1974 thesis, a claim the IEEE Neural Network Society cited when it gave him its Pioneer Award in 1994.

## Why it matters
The idea that became standard for training neural networks, sending an error signal backward through the layers, was written down more than a decade before it became widely used in 1986.
