## What happened
David Ackley, Geoffrey Hinton and Terrence Sejnowski published “A Learning Algorithm for Boltzmann Machines” in Cognitive Science, volume 9, in 1985. They described a stochastic neural network whose units update by simulated annealing, named after Ludwig Boltzmann, and a training rule that adjusts connection weights so the network can learn internal representations without a teacher for hidden units.

## Why it matters
The Boltzmann machine introduced ideas — stochastic units, energy-based learning and unsupervised discovery of features — that influenced later deep learning, including restricted Boltzmann machines used to pre-train deep networks.
