## What happened
On October 15, 2026, Shanghai AI Laboratory announced the full release of Atria Dawn, an agent model for research and engineering tasks. Its team says it can use tools and work through multiple steps; the downloadable weights are published under the MIT license.

## Why it matters
The release offers researchers and developers a model they can run and adapt themselves for tasks that go beyond answering a single question.
