## What happened
On September 22, 2026, OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Luna for the API, ChatGPT Work and Codex. It describes Luna as a faster, more affordable model for focused, high-volume work, using methods similar to GPT-6 Astra. OpenAI said its API prices were 50% lower than GPT-5.6 Luna's promotional prices.

## Why it matters
Luna brought some of the GPT-6 family's capabilities to workloads where speed and cost matter more than using the most capable model for every task.
