## What happened
Alibaba Cloud listed Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash as released to all customers in its Model Studio, and the Qwen team announced it as its next native omnimodal model. It takes text, images, audio and video in one request and returns text, over a 1-million-token context. Speech recognition covers 74 languages and 39 Chinese dialects, and a Realtime variant answers while a live audio-video stream is still arriving. Alibaba reports 69.6 on UniClawBench, one of its audio-visual agent benchmarks, and says that across 29 evaluations its average score is more than 25 per cent above Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus, with audio-visual performance close to Gemini 3.8 Flash and overall audio performance above it.

## Why it matters
Audio and video no longer have to be transcribed or captioned before a model can use them: one model watches, listens, plans and calls tools across a long recording.
