## What happened
On April 8, 2020, Facebook AI (now Meta) published the XLM-R paper, introducing a multilingual masked language model trained on 2.5 terabytes of newly created clean CommonCrawl data across 100 languages. The model uses a standard Transformer architecture and achieves state-of-the-art results on cross-lingual understanding tasks.

## Why it matters
XLM-R demonstrated that large-scale multilingual pretraining on diverse languages yields strong cross-lingual transfer performance, establishing a foundation for subsequent multilingual models and expanding AI accessibility to many languages worldwide.
