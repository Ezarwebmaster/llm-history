## What happened
On October 6, 2026, Mistral AI launched a public preview of Mistral Large 4, nicknamed "Le Chonk": a multimodal mixture-of-experts model (an architecture that activates only part of its weights for each token) with 1.05 trillion total and 52 billion active parameters, according to Mistral's documentation. Mistral says it trained the model from scratch on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in the company's own datacenters in Europe and serves the preview there. The docs list a 1M-token context window, and Mistral says much of the training data was multilingual, spanning more than 160 languages. Mistral said the downloadable weights would follow by the end of the month.

## Why it matters
Mistral calls Mistral Large 4 its largest and most capable model to date and a milestone for sovereign AI: organisations that want to run a large open-weight model under their own control and under their own laws.
