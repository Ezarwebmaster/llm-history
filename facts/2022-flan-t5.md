## What happened
On October 20, 2022, Google Research published "Scaling Instruction-Finetuned Language Models" by Hyung Won Chung and colleagues and released open-weight Flan-T5 checkpoints under Apache 2.0 in five sizes: Small (80M parameters), Base (250M), Large (780M), XL (3B), and XXL (11B). Initialized from T5 1.1 encoder-decoder models (reading input text with one network stack and generating output text with another), Flan-T5 underwent instruction finetuning (training on tasks phrased as natural-language instructions) across 1,836 tasks from 473 datasets, including nine chain-of-thought datasets (examples writing out step-by-step reasoning). On five-shot MMLU, 3B Flan-T5-XL scored 52.4%—surpassing 175B GPT-3's 43.9%—and 11B Flan-T5-XXL reached 55.1%, gaining 26.6 normalized points over untuned T5-XXL.

## Why it matters
Flan-T5 gave researchers and developers compact, Apache-licensed models that followed instructions and reasoned step by step out of the box, outperforming much larger unaligned models on accessible hardware.
