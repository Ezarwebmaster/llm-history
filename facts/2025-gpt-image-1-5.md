## What happened
On December 16, 2025, OpenAI began rolling out the new image model to all ChatGPT users and made it available to developers as `gpt-image-1.5`. The dedicated Images interface began rolling out separately, with Business and Enterprise access to that interface planned for later. The model was added to the Responses API image-generation tool on December 19.

## Why it matters
OpenAI reported faster image generation and more precise edits that better preserved lighting, composition and people's appearance. That made it easier to ask for a local change, such as replacing an object, while keeping the rest of the image consistent across successive edits.
