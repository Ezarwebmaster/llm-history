## What happened
On October 15, 2026, the Institute of Foundation Models at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) and Inception42 released Jais 3 under the Apache 2.0 open-source license. Built on the K2 Horizon open foundation model family, the suite includes six text models from 0.9 billion parameters to a 375-billion-parameter sparse mixture-of-experts model—which routes each token through only 23 billion active parameters—with context windows of 128,000 to 512,000 tokens. It ships alongside Jais Speech for transcribing Modern Standard Arabic, regional dialects across 19 countries, and mid-sentence switches into English or French.

## Why it matters
Training natively on Arabic dialects rather than translating from English preserves regional phrasing and cultural context. MBZUAI and Inception42 say their flagship model leads open models on an audited suite of 24 public Arabic benchmarks for agentic workflows.
