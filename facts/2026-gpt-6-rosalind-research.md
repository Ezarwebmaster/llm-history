## What happened
On October 30, 2026, OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Rosalind Research for multistep life-sciences research. It began rolling out to a limited set of approved research customers in Work, Codex and the API, but not in ChatGPT's Chat tab. OpenAI says the model uses the same weights as GPT-6 Astra and adds no further training for this release.

## Why it matters
The launch applies a general reasoning model to life-sciences research through a restricted-access service, pairing specialized use with additional review and safeguards.
