## What happened
On December 4, 2025, following a November safety-testing preview, Google rolled out Gemini 3 Deep Think to Google AI Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app. Using parallel reasoning to explore multiple hypotheses at once, the initial release scored 41.0% on Humanity's Last Exam without tools and 45.1% on ARC-AGI-2 with code execution. On February 12, 2026, Google released a major upgrade (evaluated as of January 2026 in DeepMind's research report on its Aletheia math agent) and opened early access via the Gemini API, reporting 48.4% on Humanity's Last Exam without tools, 84.6% on ARC-AGI-2, and a 3455 Codeforces Elo.

## Why it matters
Scaling inference-time parallel reasoning allowed a frontier model to move from Olympiad puzzles to assisting researchers with open problems in mathematics, physics, and engineering.
