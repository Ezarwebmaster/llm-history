## What happened
On November 10, 2026, the SpeakLeash Foundation and ACK Cyfronet AGH released Bielik 11B v3.1 under the Apache 2.0 license on Hugging Face. Expanding on Bielik v3.0's 32 languages through continuous pre-training on an additional 500 billion tokens, the 11-billion-parameter model supports 56 languages—including Polish, all official European Union languages, and Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Armenian, Azerbaijani, and Georgian. SpeakLeash added native function calling, structured JSON output enforcement, and step-by-step reasoning modes, alongside quantized checkpoints and the DFlash draft model for speculative decoding (predicting draft tokens ahead to speed up generation).

## Why it matters
Bielik 11B v3.1 extends a sovereign European open-weight model family from regional Polish focus into broad multilingual and tool-using deployment while remaining compact enough for local and on-premise hardware.
