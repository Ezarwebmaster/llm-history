## What happened
On November 5, 2026, the nonprofit evaluation organization METR published an assessment from an 11-week embedded study at Anthropic (August 12 to October 29, 2026). Tracking progress with the Anthropic Epoch Capabilities Index (AECI)—an internal aggregate capability benchmark—METR estimated that Anthropic improved model capabilities at 1.9 times its March 2024 to October 2025 pace (95% confidence interval: 1.5x to 2.8x). METR concluded faster training-compute growth did not explain the jump, estimating roughly 80% came from human researchers using AI assistance rather than fully autonomous AI R&D. Anthropic exercised contractual rights to redact a majority of the underlying evidence METR found most informative.

## Why it matters
The report provides empirical measurement of AI-assisted R&D speedups inside a frontier lab while highlighting verification limits when key evidence is redacted.
