## What happened
OpenAI released Sora 2 on September 30, 2025. The model generated video together with speech, background sound and sound effects. OpenAI reported improvements in physical behavior and following detailed instructions across multiple shots, while acknowledging that it still made mistakes.

Its initial deployment used an invitation-based Sora iOS app in the United States and Canada, with web access after invitation. A higher-quality Sora 2 Pro version was offered to ChatGPT Pro users on the web. API access was announced as a future plan, not part of that day's launch.

## Why it matters
Sora 2 expanded the output of text-conditioned generation to coordinated moving images and sound. It continued the Sora model lineage while adding an audiovisual capability distinct from the original video preview.
