## What happened
On November 3, 2026, Apple announced a developer preview that lets any developer connect an app to Siri AI, which Apple describes as a more capable, conversational and personal Siri. It starts with the beta versions of iOS, iPadOS, macOS and visionOS 27.3. Apps describe their content and actions to the assistant through App Intents, Apple's existing system for exposing what an app can do, and "Skills" teach Siri AI to chain several actions to answer one request. Apple says it will ship to customers in a few months, with App Store submissions opening in the spring. Siri AI is in beta in six languages, and Apple says it is currently unavailable in the EU on iOS, iPadOS and watchOS.

## Why it matters
An assistant built on language models becomes a way into other companies' apps: people ask in their own words, and the assistant acts inside the app for them.
