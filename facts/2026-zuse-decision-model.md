## What happened
Umair Mehmood Imam described three Zuse models that read a document and assign probabilities to fixed choices rather than generating an answer. The paper studied them on tasks across 27 European languages using a development set that also guided model selection.

## Why it matters
Many applications need a decision among known options. The study found that confidence errors varied more by task than by language in its comparisons, and that performance beyond the training languages depended on the underlying base model. These findings are from a single training run per model size.
