## What happened
At WESCON '63 in San Francisco, Bernard Widrow and his Stanford University team demonstrated practical applications of MADALINE (Many ADALINEs), an early multilayer artificial neural network. The researchers trained the adaptive threshold logic system on real-world pattern-recognition tasks, including isolated speech recognition of spoken words, weather forecasting from atmospheric pressure patterns, and real-time control of an unstable inverted pendulum.

## Why it matters
The demonstration showed that trainable neural networks could move beyond abstract theoretical models and solve practical engineering problems in speech processing and pattern recognition.
