## What happened
On December 19, 2024, Google released Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking Mode (`gemini-2.0-flash-thinking-exp-1219`) in public preview via the Gemini API. Built on Gemini 2.0 Flash, the experimental model uses extra computing time during inference—called test-time compute—to break prompts into step-by-step reasoning chains and display its thought process before giving an answer. Google shipped an updated preview (`gemini-2.0-flash-thinking-exp-01-21`) on January 21, 2025, and rolled Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking Experimental out to Gemini web and mobile app users on February 5, 2025, alongside a variant connected to YouTube, Search, and Google Maps. On March 13, 2025, Google added file uploads, Deep Research integration, and a 1-million-token context window for Gemini Advanced users.

## Why it matters
As Google's first public thinking model, Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking introduced visible step-by-step reasoning that Google later built into the Gemini 2.5 family.
