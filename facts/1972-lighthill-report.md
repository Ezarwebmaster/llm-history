## What happened
In July 1972, Cambridge mathematician James Lighthill finished a survey the UK Science Research Council had asked him to write. He argued that methods which worked in tiny toy worlds would not scale, because the number of possible combinations explodes as a problem grows. He was especially doubtful about robots and language programs meant to connect lab science with useful automation.

## Why it matters
Wikipedia records that the survey became the basis for Britain ending most university AI funding, an early freeze in money and confidence for the field.
