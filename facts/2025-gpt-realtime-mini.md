## What happened
At DevDay, OpenAI released `gpt-realtime-mini` for more cost-efficient speech-to-speech performance. Its model documentation describes it as a version of GPT-Realtime that handles audio and text in real time, including over WebRTC, WebSocket or SIP connections.

## Why it matters
The smaller option gave developers another way to build live voice applications after GPT-Realtime's production-oriented launch. It was a later model than the 2024 GPT-4o mini Realtime preview, which had brought a smaller voice model to the earlier beta API.
