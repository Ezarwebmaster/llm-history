## What happened
On November 9, 2026, French President Emmanuel Macron launched the Paris Call for AI Safety and Security at the Élysée Palace alongside the Paris Peace Forum. Sixteen countries—including France, Canada, Finland, the Netherlands, and Norway—and ten AI companies, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, Mistral AI, Hugging Face, Cohere, and Sakana AI, initially endorsed the declaration, alongside civil society and research groups; the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, China, Google, and Meta did not sign at launch. The text commits signatories to shared frontier risk evaluations, rapid incident-reporting channels, whistleblower protections, and developer-controlled fail-safe mechanisms able to pause or shut down models during training or use if safety thresholds are crossed.

## Why it matters
Although voluntary and without legal penalties, the agreement establishes a multilateral template tying frontier labs to explicit shutdown controls and cross-border incident alerts.
