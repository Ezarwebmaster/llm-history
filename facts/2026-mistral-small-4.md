## What happened
Mistral released Small 4 through its API and published downloadable model weights under the Apache 2.0 license. The model takes text and images as input. Developers can choose an everyday response mode or ask the same model to spend more effort reasoning through a problem.

## Why it matters
One application can use the same model to read a document image, answer a quick question, or work through a harder coding task. The downloadable weights let developers run the model on their own infrastructure and adapt it to their own tasks.
