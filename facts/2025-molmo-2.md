## What happened
On December 11, 2025, the Allen Institute for AI released Molmo 2, an open-weight family of multimodal models spanning 4B, 7B, and 8B parameters. Expanding on earlier image models, Molmo 2 natively handles video comprehension, multi-image reasoning, object tracking, and video pointing (returning spatial coordinates and timestamps). Alongside model weights, Ai2 released its training datasets, evaluation suites, and open-source codebase without relying on proprietary model distillation.

## Why it matters
Molmo 2 provides the open-source research community with fully inspectable, high-efficiency vision-language models that perform fine-grained temporal and spatial grounding in video without proprietary dependencies.
