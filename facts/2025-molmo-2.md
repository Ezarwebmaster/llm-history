## What happened
Ai2 released three Molmo 2 models with downloadable weights. Two used Qwen 3 as their language model and one used Ai2's Olmo. Ai2 also shared the datasets it built to teach the models tasks such as tracking an object across frames and answering questions about a clip.

## Why it matters
Ask how many times an action happens and the model can return a timestamp and a point in the picture for each occurrence. A viewer can jump to those moments to check the count and see which event the model missed or counted twice.
