## What happened
On December 17, 2025, Google launched Gemini 3 Flash globally in the Gemini app (replacing Gemini 2.5 Flash as the default) and AI Mode in Search, and in preview via the Gemini API in Google AI Studio, Vertex AI, Gemini Enterprise, Gemini CLI, Android Studio, and Google Antigravity. Google reports 90.4% on GPQA Diamond, 33.7% on Humanity's Last Exam without tools, 81.2% on MMMU Pro, and 78% on SWE-bench Verified, surpassing Gemini 3 Pro on that coding benchmark. Google states it adjusts thinking effort at its highest setting, using 30% fewer tokens on average than Gemini 2.5 Pro on typical traffic while running three times faster in Artificial Analysis tests.

## Why it matters
Making Gemini 3's reasoning and coding capabilities default for everyday users and fast enough for high-frequency agentic workflows narrowed the gap between flagship and lightweight tiers.
