## What happened
On 12 October 2026 Meta introduced Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, which it calls its most capable models to date. Meta made Muse Wonder available that day through the Meta Model API and Muse Code, and Muse Wonder Full only to Muse Code subscribers. The model card says the models take text, image, video and audio as input and produce text. Meta describes two features: Active Memory, carrying important context across conversations, and Active Focus, continuing to work through blockers on a long task.

## Why it matters
Meta positioned the series for agents that do multi-step personal and professional work, such as handling documents and building software, rather than for single answers. The stronger Full variant being limited to a subscription coding tool shows a split between the model people can build products on and the model they can only use directly.
