## What happened
On April 18, 2024, Meta released the initial models of Meta Llama 3 in 8-billion and 70-billion parameter sizes, available for download as pretrained and instruction-tuned checkpoints. Both models used a decoder-only transformer architecture with Grouped-Query Attention (GQA) and a 128,000-token vocabulary tokenizer, trained on a cluster of over 15 trillion tokens of publicly available data.

## Why it matters
Llama 3 substantially raised open-weight model performance on standard language, coding, and reasoning benchmarks, establishing a widely adopted open foundation for community fine-tuning and commercial deployment.
