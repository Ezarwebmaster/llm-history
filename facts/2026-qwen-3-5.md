## What happened
The Qwen team released the first model in its Qwen3.5 series with downloadable weights under the Apache 2.0 licence. It uses a mixture of experts, a design that activates only part of the model for each piece of input, and can process both text and images.

## Why it matters
The release gives developers and researchers a model they can run and adapt themselves for tasks involving both visual and written information.
