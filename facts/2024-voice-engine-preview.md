## What happened
OpenAI publicly described Voice Engine, a model for generating speech in custom voices. It said development began in late 2022 and private testing with a small group of trusted partners began in late 2023. The March announcement shared preview applications in reading assistance, translation and communication support; it did not make custom voice generation widely available.

## Why it matters
The preview separated research capability from public access. OpenAI required informed speaker consent and disclosure of generated speech, and described watermarking and monitoring. It said the technology already powered preset voices in its text-to-speech API and ChatGPT voice features, while broader custom-voice deployment remained under consideration.
