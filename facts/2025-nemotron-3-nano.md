## What happened
NVIDIA made Nemotron 3 Nano available while announcing its Nemotron 3 family. It described Nano as a 30-billion-parameter model for tasks including debugging, summarization and information retrieval. Its mixture-of-experts design activates a subset of the model for each token instead of using all its parameters. NVIDIA said Nano could process up to one million tokens of input context.

## Why it matters
The release gave developers an open model for running individual jobs within systems of cooperating AI agents. Nano was available on the announcement day; the larger Super and Ultra models were separate members of the announced family.
