## What happened
On July 16, 2026, Moonshot launched Kimi K3 on Kimi.com, its mobile apps, the Kimi Work desktop app, Kimi Code and the Kimi API. It is a mixture-of-experts model: a router sends each token to 16 of 896 specialised sub-networks, so only 104 billion of its 2.8 trillion parameters work on any one token. It reads text and images across a context of about one million tokens. Moonshot said the full weights would follow by July 27, and its repository now offers them under its own Kimi K3 License.

## Why it matters
Moonshot calls K3 "the world's first open 3T-class model", and Fortune called it the largest open-weight language model available. Moonshot also wrote that K3 still trails the strongest closed models, naming Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol.
