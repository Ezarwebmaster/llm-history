## What happened
Meta published research on GSLM2, a spoken language model that learns several complementary kinds of speech units and predicts them together. Its researchers report experiments using up to six million hours of audio and a model with 6.5 billion parameters. They released training code and model checkpoints.

## Why it matters
The work explores how a language model can learn from speech itself, rather than relying on written transcripts. Meta reports that this approach narrows the performance gap with text-based models on several tests of linguistic understanding.
