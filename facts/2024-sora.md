## What happened
OpenAI presented Sora on February 15, 2024, showing videos up to a minute long generated from text. Initial access went to safety testers and selected artists, designers and filmmakers. It was a research preview, not a public release.

Sora used a transformer, the architecture behind many language models, to process compressed pieces of images and video across space and time. A diffusion process gradually removed noise to generate the video, guided by text.

On December 9, 2024, OpenAI released the faster Sora Turbo at Sora.com for ChatGPT Plus and Pro users, initially excluding several European markets.

## Why it matters
Sora extended text-conditioned generation from still images to moving scenes. Its research demonstration and later public product involved different model versions and access conditions.
