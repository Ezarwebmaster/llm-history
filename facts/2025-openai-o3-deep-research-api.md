## What happened
OpenAI announced o3-deep-research and o4-mini-deep-research in the API on June 26, 2025. Developers could trigger research in their own workflows, using web search and connected data sources. Deep research had launched earlier as a ChatGPT capability on February 2, powered by a version of o3 optimized for browsing and analysis.

## Why it matters
The API made multi-step research available to software outside ChatGPT. It could gather and synthesize sources into a report, though OpenAI warned that deep research could invent facts or confuse authoritative information with rumors.
