## What happened
On 3 September 2026 OpenAI published a safety and system overview for GPT-6 Astra, announcing the Astra family as a broadly deployed, high-capability model. OpenAI describes Astra as more robust to jailbreaks and better aligned than prior GPT‑5.6 models, and reports a program of monitoring and deployment safeguards for higher-risk use.

## Why it matters
OpenAI positions Astra as a capability-and-safety step for frontier deployments: better alignment and monitoring change how providers and customers evaluate whether to use the most capable models in sensitive settings.
