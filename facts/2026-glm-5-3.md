## What happened
On August 14, 2026, Z.ai released GLM-5.3. It reuses GLM-5.2's base model, and Z.ai says every gain comes from further post-training, the stage after the main training run. Z.ai reports a 50% gain over GLM-5.2 on its own coding test and a Terminal-Bench 3.0 score of 28.3, up from 4.6. Z.ai said the weights would follow two weeks after launch, and a model page with them is now on Hugging Face.

## Why it matters
A Chinese lab shows that extra training alone, without a new base model, can sharply raise coding scores, and publishes the weights.
