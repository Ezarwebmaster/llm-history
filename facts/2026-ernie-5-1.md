## What happened
On May 9, 2026, Baidu released ERNIE 5.1, carved out of ERNIE 5.0 by extracting a sub-network from the larger model's elastic training, where one run trains many model sizes at once. Baidu says total parameters fell to about one third and active parameters to about half of ERNIE 5.0's, using about 6% of the pre-training compute of comparable models. Post-training added a fully asynchronous reinforcement-learning system and a four-stage pipeline that trains expert models in parallel and distills them into one model. On launch day ERNIE 5.1 scored 1,223 on the Arena Search leaderboard, placing fourth worldwide and first among Chinese models.

## Why it matters
Re-using a big model's training run to make a much cheaper one, then lifting it with reinforcement learning, had become a common route to a new top Chinese model by 2026: the contest was as much about cost per capability as about scores.
