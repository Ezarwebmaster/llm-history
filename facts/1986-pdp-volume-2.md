## What happened
MIT Press published Volume 2 of *Parallel Distributed Processing: Explorations in the Microstructure of Cognition* on July 17, 1986. James L. McClelland, David E. Rumelhart and the PDP Research Group applied connectionist models—networks of simple units whose connections carry learned information—to perception, memory, language and thought.

## Why it matters
The volume brought neural-network explanations of language and other mental processes into a wider cognitive-science discussion, part of the research lineage behind today's language models.
