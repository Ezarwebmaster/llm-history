## What happened
OpenAI announced o4-mini-deep-research alongside o3-deep-research on June 26, 2025. It was the faster option for multi-step research against public web sources or connected private data. Deep research had debuted as a ChatGPT feature on February 2 with a version of o3. On April 24, OpenAI introduced a lightweight ChatGPT mode powered by a version of o4-mini, expanding access and automatically switching queries to it after users reached their full-mode allowance.

## Why it matters
The API gave developers a faster model for building reports from multiple sources in their own software. ChatGPT's lightweight mode had already used the smaller model family to make more research runs available within the product.
