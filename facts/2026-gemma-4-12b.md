## What happened
On June 3, 2026, Google DeepMind released Gemma 4 12B (`Gemma 4 12B Unified`) in pre-trained and instruction-tuned checkpoints under Apache 2.0, followed by the Gemma 4 Technical Report on July 2, 2026. Built as an 11.95B-parameter, 48-layer dense decoder-only transformer with a 256K-token context window, it interleaves 1,024-token local sliding-window and global attention, supporting 140+ languages, step-by-step thinking, `system` prompts, and function calling. Unlike other Gemma 4 sizes with separate vision and audio encoders, Gemma 4 12B uses an encoder-free design: a 35M-parameter vision embedder projects 48x48 pixel patches via one matrix multiplication with coordinate lookups, while 16 kHz audio waveforms are sliced into 40ms frames (640 floats) and linearly projected into the token space, alongside Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) drafters for 16 GB RAM laptops.

## Why it matters
Replacing separate vision and audio encoders with direct linear projections cut multimodal latency and let developers fine-tune all modalities together in one pass.
