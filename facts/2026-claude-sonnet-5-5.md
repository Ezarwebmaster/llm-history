## What happened
Anthropic announced Claude Sonnet 5.5 on September 28, 2026, as the second model of the Claude 5.5 family after Opus 5.5. Anthropic says it runs 30% or more faster than Sonnet 5 and needs fewer tokens for the same work. It reports 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, a command-line coding test, against 10.3% for Sonnet 5. It is the first Sonnet model launched with Anthropic's cybersecurity safeguards.

## Why it matters
Anthropic positions it as the faster everyday companion to the larger Opus 5.5, which it says remains stronger on complex, open-ended work.
