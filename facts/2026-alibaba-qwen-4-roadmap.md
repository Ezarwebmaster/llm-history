## What happened
At its 2026 Apsara Conference, Alibaba Cloud outlined a full-stack AI strategy spanning chips, cloud infrastructure, models and agents. The company said its next-generation model Qwen 4 is in training, with the Qwen 4.5 and Qwen 5 series projected to scale to 5 to 10 trillion parameters. It also announced first cloud regions in Türkiye, Finland and the Netherlands, and a target of more than 20 gigawatts of data-center capacity by 2032.

## Why it matters
The roadmap signals how far Alibaba plans to grow its next models and ties them to its own chips and cloud, extending Qwen from a model family toward a full hardware-to-application stack.
