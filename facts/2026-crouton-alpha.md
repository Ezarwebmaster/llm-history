## What happened
On 3 November 2026, Crouton Alpha was listed on OpenRouter as a stealth model, operated by a third-party provider whose prompts and completions are not used for training, under OpenRouter's Stealth Model Terms. It is presented for bounded coding, agentic and document tasks alongside larger frontier models.

## Why it matters
A lightweight stealth preview lets users test a new model before its public launch, while the platform notes that its benchmark scores and provider details are not yet published.
