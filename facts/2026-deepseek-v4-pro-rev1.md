## What happened
On April 24, 2026, DeepSeek released a preview of V4 Pro and V4 Flash and published their weights. DeepSeek lists V4 Pro at 1.6 trillion total parameters with 49 billion active, and says the V4 models support a one-million-token context window.

## Why it matters
The release made DeepSeek's large model weights available to others and brought a one-million-token context window to its official services.
