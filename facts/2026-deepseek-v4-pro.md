## What happened
On April 8, 2026, DeepSeek announced V4 Pro, a mixture-of-experts model with 37 billion active parameters and 1.6 trillion total parameters. The model is released under the MIT license and supports a context window of 130,000 tokens. It includes improvements in multilingual capability and tool use support, building on the earlier V4 Flash architecture.

## Why it matters
V4 Pro represents a significant step toward truly open frontier models, offering enterprise-grade capabilities while remaining fully open-weight. Its MIT license makes it suitable for commercial deployment without restrictions, and the larger parameter count enables stronger performance on complex reasoning tasks.
