## What happened
In 1959, John McCarthy published "Programs with Common Sense," proposing an advice-taking program that would store knowledge as logical statements and derive conclusions from them. His example has it combine rules about locations, walking and driving to plan a route from a desk to an airport.

## Why it matters
The design aimed for a program that could apply general rules to new situations, rather than only follow a fixed list of instructions.
