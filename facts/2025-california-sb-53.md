## What happened
California Governor Gavin Newsom signed Senate Bill 53, the Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act, on September 29, 2025. The law requires large frontier AI developers to publish on their own websites a framework describing how they apply safety practices, and creates a channel for companies and the public to report potential critical safety incidents to California's Office of Emergency Services. It also protects whistleblowers who disclose significant health and safety risks from frontier models, and lets the state Attorney General impose civil penalties for noncompliance.

## Why it matters
It is the first law in the United States to force the companies building the most capable AI models to show their safety work in public, rather than leave it voluntary.
