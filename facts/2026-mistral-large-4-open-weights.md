## What happened
On October 29, Mistral released the weights of Mistral Large 4, a model with one trillion parameters. It had first launched the model as a public preview on October 6.

## Why it matters
The weight release lets developers download and run this large model on infrastructure they control instead of accessing it only as a hosted preview.
