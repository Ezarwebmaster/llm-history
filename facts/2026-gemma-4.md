## What happened
Google DeepMind released Gemma 4 in four variants. All four can process images and video, while the smaller E2B and E4B models also accept audio. Gemma 4 supports function calling, where a model asks an application to run a tool and can use the returned result.

## Why it matters
Google released the weights, the learned values that make up the models, under the Apache 2.0 license. Developers could run and adapt the models themselves, including deploying the smaller versions on phones and other devices with limited memory.
