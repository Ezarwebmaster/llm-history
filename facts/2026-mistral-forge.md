## What happened
Mistral introduced Forge for organizations training models on proprietary documents, code and operational records. It described support for pretraining, which teaches a model from large datasets; post-training, which refines its behavior; and reinforcement learning, which uses feedback to improve its decisions. Mistral named partners including ASML, Ericsson and the European Space Agency.

## Why it matters
Forge extended enterprise customization beyond supplying documents in a prompt: organizations could encode internal knowledge in the model through training. Mistral presented this as a way to keep control of data and model behavior while adapting models to internal workflows.
