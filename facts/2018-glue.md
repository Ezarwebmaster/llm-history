## What happened
On April 20, 2018, researchers from New York University, DeepMind, and the University of Washington published the General Language Understanding Evaluation (GLUE) benchmark. Led by Alex Wang, the team assembled nine diverse English sentence-understanding tasks, including sentiment analysis (SST-2), textual entailment (MNLI, RTE), and question answering equivalence (QQP, QNLI). GLUE provided a single public leaderboard with an evaluation service on private test splits to track general-purpose sentence representations and multi-task models.

## Why it matters
Prior evaluations measured language models on single narrow datasets. By aggregating nine distinct sentence tasks into a shared multi-task benchmark, GLUE became the standard test suite for evaluating transfer learning in models like BERT, RoBERTa, and early GPT architectures.
