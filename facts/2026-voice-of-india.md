## What happened
On April 21, 2026, researchers at IIT Madras and Josh Talks posted a paper describing Voice of India. Their test used 536 hours of unscripted phone conversations from 36,691 speakers. It accepted multiple spellings of the same spoken word, including English words written in an Indian script.

## Why it matters
A single reference spelling can make a system look as if it misheard a word it transcribed correctly. The team accounted for those variants and mapped errors by district, showing regional differences that a single score for each language could hide.
