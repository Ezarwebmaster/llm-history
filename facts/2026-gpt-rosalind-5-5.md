## What happened
OpenAI announced a new GPT-Rosalind model update. Its system card identifies GPT-Rosalind-5.5 as incrementally trained from GPT-5.5 and describes a research preview limited to trusted organizations, qualified scientists, research institutes and government partners with appropriate security and governance.

## Why it matters
The update continued the specialist series after its April introduction and before GPT-6 Rosalind Research in October. It paired a newer general model base with controlled access for life sciences research.
