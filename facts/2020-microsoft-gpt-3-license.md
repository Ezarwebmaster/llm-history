## What happened
On September 22, 2020, Microsoft announced it had exclusively licensed GPT-3, the 175-billion-parameter language model OpenAI had released that June through its API. Microsoft said it would use the model's technical innovations in its own products and services, built on the Azure supercomputer that trained it. OpenAI said the deal did not change access to GPT-3 through its API.

## Why it matters
The licence tied a leading language model to a cloud provider's commercial products, an early example of a lab and a large technology company sharing one model.
