## What happened
Zhanyu Chen and Jaap Jumelet compared versions of six model families before and after training them to follow instructions. They used MultiBLiMP, a test that pairs grammatical and ungrammatical sentences across 101 languages. The authors found that measured grammar performance declined more after instruction training for languages with less available digital text. Asking in the language under test recovered some performance in those languages.

## Why it matters
The way a test asks a question can affect how much grammatical knowledge it reveals. A model may show more of what it has learned when the instructions are in the same language as the examples.
