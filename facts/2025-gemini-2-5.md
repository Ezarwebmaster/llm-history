## What happened
On March 25, 2025, Google DeepMind introduced Gemini 2.5, which it calls a "thinking model": it works through its reasoning before giving an answer. The first release, Gemini 2.5 Pro Experimental, came out in Google AI Studio and in the Gemini app for Gemini Advanced subscribers, with Vertex AI to follow. Google said it debuted at number one on LMArena, a leaderboard built from people's votes between two answers, and reported 18.8% on Humanity's Last Exam without tools and 63.8% on SWE-bench Verified with a custom agent setup. It reads text, audio, images, video and whole code repositories, with a 1 million-token context window and 2 million announced.

## Why it matters
Google's first thinking model, Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking, had been a separate experiment; with Gemini 2.5, Google said it would build thinking into all of its models.
