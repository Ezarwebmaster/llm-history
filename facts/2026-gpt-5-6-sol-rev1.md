## What happened
On July 9, 2026, OpenAI made the GPT-5.6 family generally available after a limited preview. Sol was the flagship for complex professional work. Terra served everyday work, and Luna emphasized efficiency. The API accepted text and images and let developers choose how much effort Sol spent reasoning. Its context window held 1,050,000 tokens, with a separate limit of 128,000 tokens on output.

## Why it matters
Developers could give Sol documents and images to work through and let it call tools such as web search or a code interpreter during the same task.
