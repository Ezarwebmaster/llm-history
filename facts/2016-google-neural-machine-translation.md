## What happened
On September 27, 2016, Quoc V. Le and Mike Schuster of Google Brain announced that the Google Translate mobile and web apps now use GNMT, Google's neural machine translation system, for 100% of Chinese-to-English translations, about 18 million a day. Google says bilingual human raters found translation errors reduced by more than 55% to 85% on several major language pairs.

## Why it matters
It shows a neural translation system at work in a mass-market product, handling about 18 million translations a day, with the error figures Google published as its own measure of the gain.
