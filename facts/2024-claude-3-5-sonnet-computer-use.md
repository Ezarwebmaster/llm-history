## What happened
Anthropic released an upgraded Claude 3.5 Sonnet and made its new computer-use capability available in a public beta through the Anthropic API, Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud's Vertex AI. Developers could have the model look at a screen, move the cursor, click and type. Anthropic described the capability as experimental and said it could be cumbersome and error-prone.

## Why it matters
The beta let developers test whether a language model could operate software through interfaces already designed for people. Anthropic said Replit was exploring the ability to navigate interfaces as a way to evaluate apps while they were being built.
