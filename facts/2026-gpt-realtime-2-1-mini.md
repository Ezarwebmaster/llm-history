## What happened
OpenAI released GPT-Realtime-2.1 mini alongside GPT-Realtime-2.1 in the Realtime API. OpenAI describes the mini as a distilled model, meaning it uses training signals from a larger model to learn similar behavior. It accepts speech or text, can answer aloud, and supports calling tools supplied by the application.

## Why it matters
Both 2.1 models can power general-purpose voice assistants. OpenAI positioned mini for applications that prioritize response speed and operating cost, and the full GPT-Realtime-2.1 for workflows that need stronger reasoning, instruction following, and tool use.
