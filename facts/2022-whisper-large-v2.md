## What happened
OpenAI released the large-v2 weights on December 8, 2022. It kept the original large model's design and size, trained the model longer, and reported improvements in transcription, translation, and language identification.

## Why it matters
Developers could download the trained model and run it themselves. On March 1, 2023, OpenAI separately launched the hosted Whisper API, which used large-v2 for audio-file transcription and translation into English.
