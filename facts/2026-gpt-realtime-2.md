## What happened
OpenAI released GPT-Realtime-2 in the Realtime API. Developers could choose how much reasoning effort the voice model uses, from minimal to extra high, balancing response time against more deliberation on difficult requests. OpenAI described it as its first voice model with GPT-5-class reasoning.

## Why it matters
A voice agent could spend more effort on a complex request while retaining a faster setting for simpler exchanges. The model also supports following instructions and using tools during spoken conversations.
