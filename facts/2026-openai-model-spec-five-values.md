## What happened
On October 29, 2026, OpenAI published a new version of its Model Spec, the public document that says how its models should behave. The rules are now grouped under five values: be faithful to human intent, minimise harm, be honest and transparent, help people make their own choices, and act with care. OpenAI calls faithfulness to human intent the foundation: the model should understand what people are trying to do rather than follow their words literally. The document now calls the model "GPT", and OpenAI writes that its production models "do not yet fully reflect" it.

## Why it matters
As models act more on their own, a published rulebook lets anyone see the behaviour a lab is aiming for, and point out where its models fall short of it.
