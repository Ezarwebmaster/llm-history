## What happened
OpenAI released GPT-4o mini in the API and began replacing GPT-3.5 in ChatGPT. It supported text and image inputs at launch; audio and video support were future plans. OpenAI reported stronger academic benchmark results than GPT-3.5 Turbo, including 82% on MMLU, a test of knowledge across many subjects.

## Why it matters
A small model made frequent calls, long conversations and fast customer support more practical. Developers gained text and vision capabilities at lower cost and latency, rather than paying for a frontier model for every request. The launch improved the affordable tier over GPT-3.5 Turbo; it did not promise the full capabilities of the larger GPT-4o.
