## What happened
Weizenbaum describes ELIZA in Communications of the ACM in January 1966. Its best-known script, DOCTOR, turns the user’s own words back into questions.

## Why it matters
People confided in a program that understood nothing. The “ELIZA effect” still frames how we read chatbots.
