## What happened
On October 2, 2006, Netflix announced the Netflix Prize, offering a grand prize to anyone who could improve the accuracy of its Cinematch recommendation system (software that predicts how much a user will like a movie) by at least 10%. It published a dataset of about 100 million anonymized ratings covering 17,000 films from nearly 500,000 users, at the time the largest public rating dataset.

## Why it matters
The contest drew over 40,000 teams worldwide and popularized collaborative filtering (guessing your taste from people with similar taste), accelerating recommender systems that now shape streaming, shopping, and social feeds.
