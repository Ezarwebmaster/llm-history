## What happened
On November 9, 2026, Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, which it describes as one model with two levels of safeguards: Fable 5.2 is open to all users, while Mythos 5.2 goes only to organisations verified for cybersecurity or life-sciences work. Anthropic reports 69.9% on FrontierSWE v2 (against 56.3% for Fable 5.1), 74.6% on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 (against 52.6%) and 74.2% on Humanity's Last Exam with tools (against 65.6%), and says output is up to 30% faster than Fable 5.1. Anthropic still recommends Claude Opus 5.5 for most everyday tasks, and its documentation now lists Fable 5.1 as legacy.

## Why it matters
Anthropic says the model solved two open problems in black hole physics and that over 90% of changes merged into its own codebase are now written by Claude under human guidance: claims about what a frontier model does, not only what it scores.
