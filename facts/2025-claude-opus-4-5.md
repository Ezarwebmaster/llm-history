## What happened
Anthropic announced Claude Opus 4.5 on November 24, 2025, calling it the best model in the world for coding, agents and computer use. The release added an "effort" setting to the API, which lets developers choose between spending less time and money and getting the most capability. Anthropic says that at medium effort the model matched the best score of Claude Sonnet 4.5 on a software-engineering test while using 76% fewer output tokens.

## Why it matters
The effort setting turned "how hard should the model think" into a choice developers make for each request.
