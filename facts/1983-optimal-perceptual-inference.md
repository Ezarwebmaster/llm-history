## What happened
In June 1983, Geoffrey Hinton of Carnegie Mellon and Terrence Sejnowski of Johns Hopkins presented "Optimal Perceptual Inference" at the IEEE Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition. They proposed a network that uses randomness to weigh possible interpretations of an image and a learning rule to improve those judgments.

## Why it matters
The paper connected probabilistic reasoning with a network that could learn its own connection strengths, an early step in the development of Boltzmann machines.
