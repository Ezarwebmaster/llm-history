## What happened
OpenAI launched o1-mini alongside o1-preview. The smaller model was optimized for science, mathematics and coding and used the same reinforcement learning approach as o1, learning from feedback on its problem solving. OpenAI reported that it was faster than o1-preview, but weaker on questions requiring broad factual knowledge.

## Why it matters
The release offered a more efficient option for applications needing technical reasoning, with a trade-off in breadth. OpenAI's tests showed that a smaller model could outperform o1-preview on some mathematics and coding competitions, even while lacking some of its broader knowledge.
