## What happened
OpenAI released `gpt-realtime`, a speech-to-speech model, alongside the general availability of its Realtime API. The release added capabilities for voice applications including image input, remote tool connections and SIP phone calling. OpenAI described improvements in instruction following and function calling over its earlier voice models.

## Why it matters
The 2024 GPT-4o Realtime previews had already enabled low-latency spoken interaction. GPT-Realtime was a later model accompanying the API's move from public beta to general availability, aimed at reliable production voice agents.
