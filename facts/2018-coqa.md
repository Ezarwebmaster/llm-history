## What happened
On August 21, 2018, Stanford NLP researchers Siva Reddy, Danqi Chen, and Christopher D. Manning introduced CoQA, a benchmark for conversational question answering. The dataset gathers 127,000 questions and free-form answers from 8,000 two-person conversations about text passages across seven domains, pairing answers with passage evidence. Baseline reading comprehension models scored 65.4% F1, trailing human performance of 88.8% by 23.4 points on conversational phenomena such as coreference and pragmatic reasoning.

## Why it matters
Traditional question answering evaluated isolated queries against single passages. CoQA pushed natural language models toward multi-turn dialog understanding, requiring systems to track conversation history and resolve references across consecutive questions.
