## What happened
On December 9, 2025, Mistral AI released Devstral 2, a family of coding models in two sizes: Devstral 2, with 123 billion parameters under a modified MIT license, and Devstral Small 2, with 24 billion under Apache 2.0, small enough, Mistral says, to run on consumer hardware. Mistral reports 72.2% and 68.0% on SWE-bench Verified, a test where a model fixes real bugs in open-source projects. In human evaluations Mistral commissioned, Devstral 2 was preferred over DeepSeek V3.2, while Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 4.5 remained clearly preferred over it. Mistral also released Mistral Vibe, an open-source command-line assistant that reads, edits and runs code in a project.

## Why it matters
Mistral presents Devstral 2, five times smaller than DeepSeek V3.2, as proof that compact open models can match much larger ones at coding, while admitting that a gap with closed models remains.
