## What happened
On May 2, 2019, researchers from New York University, Facebook AI Research, DeepMind, and the University of Washington published SuperGLUE. As models like BERT surpassed non-expert human baselines on the original GLUE benchmark within a year of its release, the authors designed SuperGLUE with eight more difficult language tasks, including MultiRC, WSC, BoolQ, and ReCoRD, alongside a public leaderboard and software toolkit.

## Why it matters
SuperGLUE raised the evaluation bar for the field by emphasizing complex reasoning, coreference resolution, and causal inference. It provided a durable test bed that guided the next generation of pre-trained models, including T5 and RoBERTa.
