## What happened
Ai2's December 12 update announced Olmo 3.1 Think 32B and Instruct 32B, plus refreshed 7B RL Zero models for code and mathematics. Think continued the earlier model's training with rewards for another 21 days. Instruct applied the smaller Olmo 3 Instruct model's recipe at a larger scale for chat, tool use and dialogue. The checkpoints were offered for download, through Ai2's playground, and through partner APIs.

## Why it matters
The update extended an existing open training pipeline rather than introducing an unrelated model family. Ai2 reported improvements in mathematics, reasoning and instruction following while retaining access to the training data, code and intermediate models, allowing researchers to study how further training changes behaviour.
