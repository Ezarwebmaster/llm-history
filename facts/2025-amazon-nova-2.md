## What happened
On December 2, 2025, Amazon introduced four Nova 2 models. Nova 2 Lite, a fast reasoning model for everyday work, arrived in Amazon Bedrock that day; Nova 2 Pro, which Amazon calls its most intelligent model for complex multistep tasks, came in preview. Both let developers set low, medium or high "thinking intensity", trading speed for deeper reasoning. Nova 2 Sonic converses in speech in real time, and Nova 2 Omni takes text, images, video and speech and produces text and images. Amazon also launched Nova Forge, which it calls a first-of-its-kind service: companies mix their own data into Nova's pre-training, mid-training or post-training to build custom models it calls "Novellas".

## Why it matters
Amazon argues that companies otherwise had to customise closed models lightly, keep training open models without their original data, or build their own at great expense.
