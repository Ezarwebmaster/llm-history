## What happened
On September 19, 2024, Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen2.5, a family of open-weight dense decoder-only language models in 0.5B, 1.5B, 3B, 7B, 14B, 32B, and 72B parameter sizes alongside specialized Qwen2.5-Coder and Qwen2.5-Math variants. Pre-trained on up to 18 trillion tokens (up from 7 trillion for Qwen2) and post-trained with over 1 million supervised fine-tuning samples and multistage reinforcement learning, the models support a 128,000-token context window, up to 8,000 tokens of generation, and more than 29 languages. All open-weight sizes except the 3B and 72B variants were released under the Apache 2.0 license, with further details published in a December 19, 2024 arXiv technical report.

## Why it matters
Scaling pre-training to 18 trillion tokens across seven dense model sizes gave developers a versatile open-weight foundation from edge deployment to 72B-scale reasoning, coding, and structured data output.
