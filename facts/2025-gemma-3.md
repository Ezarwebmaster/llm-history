## What happened
On March 12, 2025, Google DeepMind released Gemma 3 (1B, 4B, 12B, and 27B) alongside the 4B ShieldGemma 2 image safety classifier, followed by its technical report on March 25, 2025. The 4B–27B variants process text and images via a 400M-parameter SigLIP vision encoder (which aligns images with text) with a 128K-token context window; the 1B model is text-only with 32K context. To curb KV-cache memory (stored attention state for long prompts), Gemma 3 alternates five 1,024-token local sliding-window layers per global attention layer. Trained via knowledge distillation (learning from a teacher model's probabilities) on 2T–14T tokens across 140+ languages, it also shipped quantized weights and reached 1338 Elo on Chatbot Arena (27B).

## Why it matters
Google DeepMind built Gemma 3 to bring long-context multimodal capabilities to a single GPU, TPU, or local device.
