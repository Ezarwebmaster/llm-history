## What happened
On February 19, 2026, Google announced Gemini 3.1 Pro across its consumer, enterprise, and developer platforms. Built as an upgraded core intelligence following Gemini 3 Deep Think, Gemini 3.1 Pro rolled out in preview through the Gemini API in Google AI Studio, Gemini CLI, Google Antigravity, and Android Studio, as well as Vertex AI, Gemini Enterprise, the Gemini app, and NotebookLM. The model features up to a 1-million-token input context window and 64,000 output tokens. Google reported a verified score of 77.1% on the ARC-AGI-2 reasoning benchmark, more than double the performance of Gemini 3 Pro.

## Why it matters
Achieving 77.1% on ARC-AGI-2 demonstrated significant progress in novel pattern synthesis and abstract reasoning for complex agentic problem-solving and software engineering.
