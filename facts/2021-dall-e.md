## What happened
OpenAI presented DALL·E, a 12-billion-parameter version of GPT-3 trained on paired text and images to generate images from captions. Its examples combined concepts, such as an armchair in the shape of an avocado.

## Why it matters
DALL·E extended a language-model approach to generating images conditioned on natural-language instructions. It was a distinct milestone from CLIP's matching of text and images: DALL·E created images from descriptions.
