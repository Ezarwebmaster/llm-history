## What happened
In September 2026, Anthropic introduced Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1. It says the two are the same model with different levels of safeguards: Fable 5.1 is generally available, while Mythos 5.1 is offered only through trusted access programs for cybersecurity and life-sciences work. Anthropic reports that Fable 5.1 scores 60.9% on Humanity's Last Exam without tools, against 57.8% for Fable 5; 55.8% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, against 42.0%; and 1853 on GDPval-AA v2, against 1723.

## Why it matters
Anthropic says the model is built for long-running coding, knowledge work and scientific research, and that it lowered the cost of reusing cached context. Releasing a generally available model next to a restricted twin for sensitive work is a way to give vetted users more capability without opening it to everyone.
