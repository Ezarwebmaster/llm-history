## What happened
The organizers made training texts available to registered teams on August 25 and released the test texts on September 25. The new task gave systems text already split into words and asked them to identify each word's grammatical role. Across all three tasks, 28 groups submitted results.

## Why it matters
Written Chinese generally omits spaces between words. The organizers scored word boundaries separately for words seen during training and for unfamiliar ones. Readers could see how well a system recognized words beyond its training vocabulary, even when its overall score was high.
