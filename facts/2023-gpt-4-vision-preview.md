## What happened
On November 6 at DevDay, OpenAI made GPT-4 Turbo's image input available under the API model name `gpt-4-vision-preview`. This followed the September 25 start of the ChatGPT image rollout for Plus and Enterprise users. OpenAI later made GPT-4 Turbo with Vision generally available in the API on April 9, 2024.

## Why it matters
The preview changed what developers could send in a request. An application could ask for a photo caption or a written explanation of a figure directly from the image. The separate GPT-4 Turbo preview announced at DevDay expanded how much text a request could hold.
