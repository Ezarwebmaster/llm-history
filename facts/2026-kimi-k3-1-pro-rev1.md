## What happened
On October 23, 2026, Moonshot introduces Kimi K3.1 Pro, the first model in its K3.1 series and a post-trained version of Kimi K3. Moonshot calls it its most capable 3T-class open-weight model to date, with a 1-million-token context window. Moonshot reports 65.7% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, a coding benchmark, ahead of GPT-6 Astra at 58.2% and behind Claude Opus 5.5 at 66.4%. It says full weights will follow by Nov. 4.

## Why it matters
Moonshot presents K3.1 Pro as a near-frontier open-weight model. The Kimi K3 blog says Kimi models set the upper bound of open-model sizes for nine of the past twelve months, so Moonshot was not absent from the open-model race.
