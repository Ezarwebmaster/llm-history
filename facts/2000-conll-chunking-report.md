## What happened
Erik F. Tjong Kim Sang of the University of Antwerp and Sabine Buchholz of Tilburg University posted a report on a shared task for text chunking: grouping related words into phrases that do not overlap. The task used Wall Street Journal text from the Penn Treebank, with phrase labels extracted from its grammatical analyses. The report described the dataset, participating systems and their results.

## Why it matters
The shared task extended evaluation beyond noun phrases to other phrase types. Its common data and scoring let researchers compare ways of finding sentence structure without building a complete grammatical analysis.
