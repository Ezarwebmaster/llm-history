## What happened
NotebookLM began on July 12, 2023 as an experiment from Google Labs: a notebook whose language model answers from the documents you give it. On June 8, 2026, Google upgraded it to Gemini 3.5 and gave it agent abilities. Each notebook now has a secure cloud computer where NotebookLM can write and run code, and it can use Google Search to find and add sources. It can produce Excel and PowerPoint files, PDFs, charts and images. In Google's own side-by-side tests, it beat the previous version over 65% of the time on average. It rolled out on the web to Google AI Ultra subscribers and Workspace business customers with AI Expanded Access, with others to follow.

## Why it matters
A tool built to answer only from the sources you gave it can now find new ones on the web and run its own analysis.
