## What happened
OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Sol for everyday coding and work that involves several tool calls. It released GPT-6.2 Astra alongside Sol for more demanding coding and professional projects, including work that follows a supplied slide or spreadsheet template. Both models were available at launch through ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API.

## Why it matters
OpenAI reports that Sol scored within half a point of Astra on FrontierCode 2.0, which grades whether changes to real codebases meet maintainers' standards for merging. It also reports stronger performance from Astra on GDPval v2, its test of multistep assignments drawn from real professional projects. The two results show how the gap between the models depends on the task.
