## What happened
On August 10, 2026, OpenAI announced GPT-5.6 Cyber alongside an expansion of its Daybreak program. Access was through Daybreak Red, the program's tier for approved defenders using purpose-trained cybersecurity models. The announcement distinguished this tier from Daybreak Blue, which provided access to general-purpose models for defensive work.

## Why it matters
This release supplies the earlier milestone behind the timeline's existing GPT-6 Cyber entry. OpenAI's November 5, 2026 GPT-6 Cyber announcement explicitly compared the newer model with GPT-5.6 Cyber, placing the August release in the same specialized model lineage.
