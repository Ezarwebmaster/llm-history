## What happened
On August 14, 2026, Hugging Face published an analysis of open-source language models from January to August 2026. Public model repositories on the Hugging Face Hub reached 2.96 million, while datasets crossed 1 million. The report showed that several Chinese labs (including Moonshot, MiniMax, Xiaomi, and Z.ai) routinely released frontier models exceeding 700 billion and clearing a trillion parameters under open licenses, while hardware vendors like NVIDIA and AMD became leading publishers of open models to demonstrate hardware performance.

## Why it matters
Open weights reached frontier scale and shifted from smaller experimental models to multi-trillion parameter deployments supported by community quantization layers, transforming how developers and enterprises adopt foundation models.
