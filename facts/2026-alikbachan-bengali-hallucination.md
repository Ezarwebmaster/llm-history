## What happened
Asif Shahriyar Sushmit and collaborators posted a preprint describing two Bengali test sets for detecting model errors, with 2,516 and 5,000 items. The benchmark groups examples by task, type of error, and how much local cultural knowledge they require. The authors compared language models used as judges with systems submitted to an open competition.

## Why it matters
The authors found that judges and most competition systems struggled to spot missing information across a passage. The two test sets also yielded different rankings, showing how the mix of questions can affect which system appears strongest.
