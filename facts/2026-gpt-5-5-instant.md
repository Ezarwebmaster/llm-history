## What happened
OpenAI began replacing GPT-5.3 Instant with GPT-5.5 Instant as the ChatGPT default across free and paid plans. The API exposed the model through the moving `chat-latest` alias. OpenAI reported more accurate and concise answers, improvements in interpreting uploaded images, and better judgment about when to search the web.

## Why it matters
The April GPT-5.5 launch focused on demanding work such as coding and research. The May rollout updated the model people encountered by default for everyday conversations. OpenAI also began expanding how ChatGPT could draw on past conversations and show the sources it used to personalize an answer.
