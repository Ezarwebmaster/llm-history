## What happened
On 3 November 2026 India's IndiaAI Mission, under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), released two sets of foundation models — large language models meant to be adapted to many tasks — at a New Delhi event. Soket Labs' EKA-1 has 26.3 billion parameters; MeitY says it was trained on about 7.5 trillion tokens, spans all 22 scheduled Indian languages plus English, and is an open model built for maths, code, reasoning and agent tasks. ZenteiQ's Dhir 2B and Dhir 5B are described as open-source assistants for English and 10 Indian languages, built to run on-premise and offline. Both teams were selected under the IndiaAI Innovation Centre pillar.

## Why it matters
The release shows a government paying for home-grown language models that serve its own languages, instead of leaving the field to a few mostly English-language commercial labs. MeitY frames it as a step toward technological self-reliance.
