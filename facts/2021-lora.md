## What happened
On June 17, 2021, Edward J. Hu, Yelong Shen, Phillip Wallis, Zeyuan Allen-Zhu, Yuanzhi Li, Shean Wang, Lu Wang, and Weizhu Chen at Microsoft submitted "LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models" to arXiv. Full fine-tuning of large models requires updating and storing every parameter for each downstream task. LoRA freezes the pretrained model weights and injects trainable rank decomposition matrices into Transformer attention layers, reducing trainable parameters by 10,000 times and GPU memory requirements by 3 times for GPT-3 175B.

## Why it matters
Because the adapter matrices can be merged with base model weights during deployment without adding inference latency, LoRA became the dominant parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) standard across open-weight language models.
