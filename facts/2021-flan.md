## What happened
On September 3, 2021, Google Research published "Finetuned Language Models Are Zero-Shot Learners" by Jason Wei and colleagues, introducing FLAN (Finetuned Language Net). While large pretrained models like GPT-3 relied primarily on few-shot prompting, FLAN demonstrated instruction tuning: finetuning a 137-billion-parameter pretrained decoder-only language model on 62 natural language processing datasets grouped into 12 task clusters using natural language prompt templates. When evaluated on unseen task clusters held out during training, FLAN outperformed its untuned 137B baseline on all tasks and surpassed zero-shot 175B GPT-3 on 20 of 25 evaluated benchmarks.

## Why it matters
FLAN established instruction tuning as a foundational technique for aligning language models with user intent, proving that training on task instructions generalizes effectively to unseen tasks without few-shot exemplars.
