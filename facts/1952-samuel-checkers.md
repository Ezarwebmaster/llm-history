## What happened
Arthur L. Samuel wrote a checkers-playing program for the IBM 701 in 1952. Samuel later extended the program with learning procedures; his 1959 paper documents the early 1950s work and describes how the program improved by playing and generalizing from experience.

## Why it matters
Samuel's work is an early, well-documented demonstration that a program could improve its play through experience, and his checkers research is often cited as a foundation for later machine learning and reinforcement-learning experiments.
