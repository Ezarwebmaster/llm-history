## What happened
On 31 December 2020, EleutherAI researchers submitted a paper describing The Pile, an 825 GiB English text corpus assembled from 22 diverse high-quality sources, many drawn from academic or professional material. The paper reports that models trained on The Pile improve over Raw CC and CC-100 web data on every Pile component and on downstream evaluations, and the group published the construction code. The dataset is public and was later used to train GPT-Neo; it was not a model release.

## Why it matters
Training-data quality and mix were becoming a lever for model quality. The Pile gave researchers a documented, freely available alternative to undifferentiated web crawls, and became a shared reference dataset for open language-model work.
