## What happened
Peters and six co-authors at Ai2 and the University of Washington posted "Deep contextualized word representations" to arXiv. The paper introduces ELMo, short for Embeddings from Language Models: word vectors are built from the internal states of a deep bidirectional language model, one that reads text left to right and right to left, pre-trained on a large text corpus. The authors show that downstream models improve across six tasks including question answering, textual entailment and sentiment analysis.

## Why it matters
A word's representation started to carry its context, not just its identity. ELMo made pre-training a bidirectional language model and then adapting it the standard recipe for sentence tasks.
