## What happened
Cohere made North Small Translate available through its Chat V2 API and released downloadable model weights for noncommercial use. The model uses a mixture of experts, activating only a subset of its components when generating each piece of text. Cohere developed it with translation company RWS.

## Why it matters
North Small Translate was the first translation model in Cohere’s North family. Its downloadable weights let researchers evaluate it and translate documents on their own infrastructure under the noncommercial license.
