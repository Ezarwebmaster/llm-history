## What happened
On March 4, 2026, Microsoft Research released Phi-4-reasoning-vision-15B, a model whose weights anyone can download. It takes pictures and text and writes text. Microsoft says it is aimed at visual math and science problems, and at finding buttons and words on screens, while staying small enough to run without a huge computer.

## Why it matters
While many vision models were growing larger and slower, a major lab still shipped a small downloadable model for documents, charts, and screens.
