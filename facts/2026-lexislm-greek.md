## What happened
Konstantinos Perifanos and collaborators reported LexisLM-500M and LexisLM-1B, trained from scratch on Greek text. The researchers evaluated them on Greek translations of several benchmarks and reported above-chance performance on ARC-Easy, while performance on ARC-Challenge and MMLU remained near chance.

## Why it matters
The work explores training smaller models specifically for Greek, a language less commonly centered in large language model development. Its results also show the limits of the models on harder evaluations.
