## What happened
On August 6, 2026, South Korean artificial intelligence company Upstage launched Solar Pro 4, succeeding Solar Pro 3 as its flagship agent-first model. Upstage designed the model for multi-step agent workflows, terminal tool automation, coding, and enterprise document workflows. Solar Pro 4 provides a 512,000-token context window with support for up to 128,000 output tokens, configurable reasoning-effort settings, and multilingual optimization across Korean, English, and Japanese.

## Why it matters
Solar Pro 4 expands frontier-class agentic capabilities in East Asia, offering long-context reasoning and low-latency tool execution tailored for enterprise automation.
