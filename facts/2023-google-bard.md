## What happened
On February 6, 2023, Google CEO Sundar Pichai announced Bard, an experimental conversational AI service that draws on information from the web. It ran on a lightweight version of LaMDA, Google's dialogue model, which Google said needs much less computing power, so it could serve more users. Bard opened that day to trusted testers. On March 21, 2023, Google began opening it to the public in the US and the UK, calling it an early experiment. On February 8, 2024, Bard was renamed Gemini, after Google's newer model family.

## Why it matters
Bard put Google's dialogue research, from LaMDA on, in front of the public, and it grew into the Gemini app.
