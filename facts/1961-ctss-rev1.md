## What happened
In November 1961, MIT demonstrated the Compatible Time-Sharing System (CTSS). It let several people use an IBM 7090 mainframe through terminals, while the Computer History Museum describes CTSS as one of the systems developed to share computer time among many users.

## Why it matters
CTSS helped show that a single mainframe could support interactive work by many people, an approach that shaped later time-sharing systems.
