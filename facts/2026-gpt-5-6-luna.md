## What happened
On July 9, 2026, OpenAI launched GPT-5.6, a family comprising Sol, Terra and Luna. OpenAI describes Luna as its fastest and most affordable model for cost-sensitive, high-volume work; its API documentation lists text and image input, tool use and adjustable reasoning effort.

## Why it matters
Luna made a lower-cost, fast model an explicit part of OpenAI's frontier family, giving high-volume applications a different point on the capability-speed-cost trade-off.
