## What happened
Hongjin Su and collaborators posted ToolOrchestra on arXiv on November 26, 2025. Their method uses reinforcement learning, rewarding successful answers, efficiency and adherence to users' tool preferences. It produced an eight-billion-parameter model called Orchestrator that coordinates other models and tools. The authors reported 37.1% on Humanity's Last Exam, compared with 35.1% for GPT-5 in their evaluation.

## Why it matters
The work explored improving a system by teaching a smaller model when to consult specialized resources. Its reported results suggested that coordinating available tools could improve difficult question answering without making the coordinating model itself larger.
