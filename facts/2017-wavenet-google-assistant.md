## What happened
DeepMind said on October 4, 2017 that an improved WaveNet was now producing the Google Assistant's two US English voices and its Japanese voice, on every platform. WaveNet builds speech one audio sample at a time instead of stitching together recorded fragments. The new version ran 1,000 times faster than the 2016 research model, taking 50 milliseconds to make one second of speech, at 24,000 samples a second and 16-bit depth. In listening tests scored from 1 to 5, the first US English voice went from 4.186 to 4.347 and the Japanese voice from 4.072 to 4.236; recorded human speech scored 4.667.

## Why it matters
Generating raw sound with a neural network stopped being a research demo and became something millions of people heard every day.
