## What happened
On May 19, 2026, AI Singapore released SEA-LION v4.5, including Qwen-SEA-LION-v4.5-27B-IT under an open MIT license. Built upon the Qwen3.6-27B dense multimodal architecture, the model was distilled on curated Southeast Asian instruction data across English and regional languages including Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, Filipino, Malay, Burmese, and Tamil. The release features a 262,144-token context window, unified vision-language processing, and reasoning capabilities tailored to Southeast Asian linguistic nuances.

## Why it matters
Most global foundation models underperform on Southeast Asian languages due to severe training data imbalances. By open-sourcing models pre-trained and instruction-tuned specifically on regional corpora, AI Singapore provides culturally grounded AI infrastructure for over 650 million people.
