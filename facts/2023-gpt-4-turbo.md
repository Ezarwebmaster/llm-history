## What happened
OpenAI launched a developer preview of GPT-4 Turbo at DevDay. Its context window could fit 128,000 tokens, or pieces of text. OpenAI estimated that the equivalent of more than 300 pages of text could fit in a single prompt.

## Why it matters
Developers could include long reports or several related documents in one request. That made it possible to ask for a summary covering the whole text or compare passages across documents without first splitting the material into separate requests.
