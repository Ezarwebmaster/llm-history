## What happened
John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, Nathaniel Rochester and Claude Shannon sign a proposal asking for a two-month, ten-person study at Dartmouth College in the summer of 1956. It lists the problems they want machines to tackle, including using language, forming abstractions, solving kinds of problems reserved for humans, and improving themselves.

## Why it matters
The proposal names the field and sets the research questions that the rest of this timeline answers.
