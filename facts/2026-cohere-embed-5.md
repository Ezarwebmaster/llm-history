## What happened
Cohere released Embed 5 Pro and Embed 5 Fast, a family of embedding models: instead of generating text, they turn text, images and documents into numeric vectors so software can search and retrieve similar content, the step a chatbot often takes before answering from a company's own documents. Cohere said Pro is built for maximum quality on complex enterprise data and Fast for high-volume, latency-sensitive retrieval, both covering more than 100 languages with a 128,000-token context window. Cohere reported that Embed 5 Pro reached the highest average score it measured on the ViDoRe V3 enterprise-document benchmark, and ranked first on three finance-specific benchmarks it tested: FinanceBench, FinQA and ViDoRe V3 Finance.

## Why it matters
Retrieval, not just generation, is what lets a language model answer from a company's own documents, and Cohere keeps competing there even as chat-model attention goes elsewhere.
