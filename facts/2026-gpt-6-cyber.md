## What happened
On November 5, 2026, OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Cyber, a model for authorized cybersecurity research. OpenAI says it improves on GPT-5.6 Cyber at finding and fixing vulnerabilities and at respecting a researcher's scope. In OpenAI's tests, it scored 48.5% on ExploitGym, compared with 32.1% for GPT-5.6 Cyber, and avoided exploiting out-of-scope services in 99.93% of tests without a deployment prompt.

## Why it matters
GPT-6 Cyber is a specialized model release built around both offensive testing ability and limits on where that ability can be used.
