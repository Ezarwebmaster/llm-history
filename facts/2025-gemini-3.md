## What happened
On November 18, 2025, Google announced Gemini 3 and released Gemini 3 Pro in preview across the Gemini app, AI Studio, Vertex AI, AI Mode in Search and the new Antigravity developer platform. Google called it its most intelligent model: it tops the LMArena leaderboard at 1501 Elo, scores 37.5% on Humanity's Last Exam without tools and 91.9% on GPQA Diamond, and reaches 76.2% on SWE-bench Verified. It has a 1 million-token context window and takes text, images, video, audio and code. A stronger reasoning mode, Gemini 3 Deep Think, was still in safety testing for Google AI Ultra subscribers.

## Why it matters
For the first time Google shipped a Gemini model into Search on day one, and Gemini 3 set the state of the art on several reasoning and multimodal benchmarks while making agentic coding a headline feature.
